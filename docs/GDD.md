# Tower Clash — Game Design Document

Working title: **Tower Clash** (a Tower War–style capture-the-towers RTS). Platform: browser (mobile-first, portrait, touch + mouse). Engine: none — TypeScript + HTML5 Canvas 2D. Source lives in `tower-clash/`.

## 1. Vision

A one-thumb real-time strategy game where every decision is simple arithmetic and every level is under three minutes. The player captures every enemy tower on a map by opening **streams** from its towers along straight, unobstructed lanes (§2.0b), choosing between letting a tower fill (it upgrades itself when full), expanding, and defending.

Design pillars:
1. **Readable** — numbers on towers, colour = owner, nothing hidden (every stream, friendly or hostile, is a visible ribbon).
2. **Tense** — a streaming tower stops growing (its number freezes while it sends), and a tower under fire recruits nothing; the AI punishes greed.
3. **Escalating vocabulary** — a new building or hazard roughly every 8 levels (§3).
4. **Short & replayable** — 3-star clock per level, plus one Daily Challenge a day (§7).

Rules history: v1 (2026-09-13: send half/all, paid upgrades), **v2** (2026-09-15: streams + auto-upgrade, §2.0), **v2.1** (2026-09-17: under fire, §2.0), **v3** (2026-09-21: free straight lanes, obstacles, streams carry production and the garrison never drains, §2.0b). §2.1–§2.7 and §4 below are written against v3 (consistency sweep 2026-09-22); where an older sentence is kept for context it is marked "(v1, superseded by §2.0)" or "(v2, superseded by §2.0b)".

## 2. Core rules (authoritative — engineers implement exactly this)

## 2.0 Rules v2 (2026-09-15, stakeholder request) — supersede §2.2 "Upgrade" and §2.3 "Sending units"

**Auto-upgrade.** Towers no longer upgrade by spending units. Capacity ladder is **25 / 50 / 100** for L1 / L2 / L3. When a L1 or L2 tower's garrison reaches its capacity it upgrades instantly (level +1, garrison kept, `upgrade` event). L3 is the maximum: the garrison caps at 100 and production stops adding. Fortress: capacity ×1.5 (37 / 75), max L2. Artillery and tank factory use the same ladder (weight for tanks). Commander "capacity" upgrades multiply the ladder; the `upgrade` command is ignored (kept for save/replay compatibility). Production intervals per level stay 1.0 / 0.7 / 0.5 s.

**Attack streams (links).** Sending is a persistent **link** from an owned tower to a road-connected tower:
- `link { owner, from, to }` — valid when `owner` owns `from`, an uncut road connects them, `to ≠ from`, and `from` has fewer than `maxLinks(level) = level` active links (L1 = 1 target, L2 = 2, L3 = 3). `unlink { owner, from, to? }` removes one or all links of `from`.
- While a tower has ≥ 1 link it **drains**: every `LEAVE_INTERVAL_MS` (120 ms) one unit (infantry, or a whole tank when ≥ 5 weight) leaves into the next link round-robin, as long as garrison ≥ 1. Newly produced and newly arrived units are drained the same way, so a linked tower does not grow and cannot auto-upgrade ("inkişafı dayanır").
- A link ends automatically when: the source changes owner; the road is cut; or the target is owned by the link owner **and** its garrison is at capacity (nothing to reinforce). A link does **not** end on capture — it keeps flowing as a supply line until the player stops it or the target fills up.
- Units on a road still clash as before; arrivals reinforce (friendly) or damage/capture (hostile) exactly as in §2.3.
- Boosters, mines, barriers, bridges, artillery are unchanged. Continue/rewind snapshots include links.

**Interaction.** Tap own tower = select. Tap a connected tower = create link (or remove it if that link exists). At the link limit the tap is refused with a short shake and hint "L2 needed for 2 streams". Drag from tower to tower = link. Tap the selected tower again = deselect. The send-ratio toggle is removed.

**Presentation.** Every active link is drawn as a road-following ribbon in the **owner's colour** (player blue, enemies red/green/yellow), ~10 px wide, 55 % alpha, with chevrons animating toward the target and an arrowhead at the target; the player's own links are brighter with a thin ink outline; enemy links are visible too so incoming attacks can be read at a glance. Tower sprites change with level: L1 small tower, L2 taller with a second storey and banner, L3 large keep with double roof and battlements (all kinds), plus the existing gems.

**AI.** Bots issue `link`/`unlink` instead of `sendUnits`: an attack is a link; "reserve" becomes "unlink when the source is threatened or the goal is reached"; the reference player unlinks a supply line when the target is full or when the source needs to grow to the next level. Exact rules, constants and the threat model: §2.5.

**Implementation notes (verified 2026-09-15 against commit 4bb3944, `src/sim/step.ts`).** These are the precise readings of the paragraphs above; where they add a number, that number is the rule.
- Tick order (50 ms): boosters expire → generation (+ auto-upgrade) → `pruneLinks` → `drain` → legacy queues → movement → hazards → clashes → artillery → arrivals (+ auto-upgrade on reinforcement) → `pruneLinks` again → outcome. The second prune means a target filled by this tick's arrivals releases its supply lines on the same tick.
- **Drain.** A tower with ≥ 1 link accumulates `drainAccMs`; every 120 ms one unit leaves into `links[linkCursor]` and the cursor advances (round-robin in link creation order). A tank factory sends a whole tank (weight 5) while it holds ≥ 5, otherwise one infantry. If the garrison is smaller than the next unit, the timer stays armed at exactly one interval, so a unit that is produced or arrives later leaves on the tick it appears. With 0 links the timer resets to 0. Consequence: a burst of `S` units takes `S × 0.12 s` to leave whatever the level; two links share it 1:1, three links 1:1:1.
- **Production does not stop while linked — growth does.** A linked tower keeps producing at its level's rate (L1/L2/L3 barracks = 1.0 / 1.43 / 2.0 units/s) and every new unit is drained on the same tick, so after the burst a stream delivers exactly the source's production plus whatever friendly streams pour into the source (streams chain through a linked tower). `tryAutoUpgrade` returns false for a linked tower: a linked L1 at 25 stays L1 until it is unlinked and its garrison reaches 25 again unlinked. At max level (L3, fortress L2) an unlinked garrison caps at capacity and production stops adding.
- **Auto-unlink** emits `unlinked { owner, from, to, reason }`, `reason ∈ { manual, sourceLost, roadCut, targetFull }`: `sourceLost` when the source changed owner (a capture removes the old owner's outgoing links inside the arrival, before the new owner can act); `roadCut` when the road is cut or gone (the `cutBridge` command removes links on that road at once, walking units on it die); `targetFull` when the target is the link owner's and `units ≥ capacity` (modified capacity for the player). A link into a neutral or hostile tower never ends by itself.
- **Link limit** is `LINKS_PER_LEVEL[level] = level` for every kind; a fortress (max L2) therefore runs at most 2 streams. The `link` command is ignored (no event) when the limit is reached, the road is cut/missing, the owner is wrong, or the identical link already exists.
- **Level 1 lesson** ("Tap your tower, then tap a target: the stream keeps flowing until you stop it") matches the code: a link into a neutral flows through the capture and keeps supplying the captured tower until the player unlinks it or the target fills (`targetFull`).

### Rules v2.1 (proposed 2026-09-15, sim implemented 2026-09-17) — "Under fire"

**Problem (headless evidence).** All-L3 endgames stalemate with every tower at 100 and nobody attacking; level 40 seed 1 times out at 180 s with the player at 8 towers vs 4 (`npm run playtest -- --level 40 --seeds 5` → 4/5). The arithmetic under v2 (measured headless, 240 px road): a capture needs damage **strictly greater** than the garrison; a linked source drains at 8.33/s while still producing, so a 100-unit L3 empties in 15.6 s and lands ≈ 130 units, during which the target regenerates ≈ 30 — it bottoms out at exactly 0 and is not flipped; from then on the trickle (the source's production) equals the target's production. Even a 99-unit L3 holds; an unanswered L1 25-stream only dents an L3 keep to 78. So one stream can never flip an equal tower, and the AI (which computes exactly this) correctly never attacks.

**Candidates evaluated.**
| Candidate | Readability on a phone | Tower War feel | AI can exploit it | Star clock | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Overflow decay: max-level tower at capacity loses 1 unit / 3 s above 90 % | Invisible: an L3 refills the lost unit in 0.5 s, the number flickers 100/99 | None | No capture threshold changes; making it bite needs production to stay paused, which only moves the stalemate from 100 to 90 | None | Rejected |
| Sudden death: after 4 min production stops everywhere, garrisons −1 / 2 s | Good (HUD countdown) | Weak — a timer, not a tactic | Yes, but only after 4 min: outside every star clock and the 180 s playtest budget; a human waits minutes doing nothing; a mirror match still ends 0 vs 0 | None | Rejected as main rule |
| Stream bonus: a stream running ≥ 10 s gets +1 unit per 3 arrivals | Breaks pillar 1: the number you sent is not the number that lands | None | Yes (keep streams running) | Small | Rejected |
| Enemy link limit = level (like the player) | n/a (AI only) | n/a | Lets an enemy split streams; does nothing for the 100-vs-100 arithmetic and nothing for the player's side | None | Folded into the AI acceptance criteria below |
| **Under fire**: a tower that is being hit does not recruit | The tower's number stops climbing and drops with every hit while a hostile ribbon points at it; no new UI | Strong: Tower War is "answer every incoming stream or lose the tower" | Yes: any sustained stream now wins in finite time, so the attack test becomes `burst + trickle × wait > garrison` | Levels resolve faster (re-measure) | **Chosen** |

**Rule.**
- New per-tower field `underFireUntilMs` (in `GameState`, included in continue/rewind snapshots). When a hostile unit **lands** on an owned tower (arrival with `unit.owner ≠ tower.owner`, whether or not it flips the tower), set `underFireUntilMs = time + UNDER_FIRE_MS`, **`UNDER_FIRE_MS = 1500`**.
- While `time < underFireUntilMs` the tower **generates nothing**: its `genAccMs` does not accumulate (the same mechanism as Freeze; Overdrive multiplies a paused rate, still 0). Everything else is unchanged: it drains its own links, accepts friendly reinforcements (each landing friendly unit is +1 and still counts toward the auto-upgrade), fires artillery, uses the fortress accumulator, can be linked/unlinked.
- A capture clears `underFireUntilMs` (the new owner starts fresh); the old owner's units still landing are hostile to the new owner and re-arm it.
- Not landings: units that die on the road (clash, mine, barrier, artillery, bridge cut). Artillery therefore counters a trickle it can kill. Neutral towers are unaffected (they never generate).
- Why 1500 ms: any barracks trickle keeps a tower under fire (landings ≤ 1.0 s apart at L1), while a tank factory alone (one landing / 4 s) or an L1 artillery post (2.0 s) does not.
- Resulting arithmetic: from the first landing the target regenerates nothing, so a stream flips a garrison `G` when its `G + 1`-th unit lands — units sent = source garrison + its production × time, one every 120 ms while the garrison lasts. 100 vs 100 (240 px road): the 101st unit leaves at 12.1 s and lands at ≈ 14 s (v2: never). An unanswered L1 25-stream (burst 25, then 1/s) kills an L3 keep at ≈ 78 s (v2: dents it to 78). A defender answers a hostile ribbon by (in this order) reinforcing from a neighbour (a supply stream out-delivers any trickle from a lower level), counter-streaming from **another** tower at the attacker's drained source (it is at 0 and under no fire), or counter-streaming from the sieged tower itself only when its garrison exceeds what is still coming (`linkPending` + weight on the road) — units meet on the road 1:1 and the remainder flips the empty source.
- Mirror-match caveat: two equal towers streaming at each other annihilate on the road and both sit at 0 for ever — under v2, v2.1 and every candidate above (the fixed point is symmetric). v2.1 makes the first mover win a mirror match when the other side does not counter-stream within the travel time. Shipped levels are never mirror matches (§3).
- Presentation (Tech Artist, shipped 3e4a388): while under fire the badge turns alert paper with a thicker stroke in the **attacker's** colour and a crossed-swords pip before the number (shape, not only colour); each landing jolts the badge and drops an impact ring (static tint only under reduced motion) — `docs/ART_DIRECTION.md` §3. Tutorial (Level Designer, shipped 1750354): level 2's lesson reads "Take the middle and keep supplying it from home: a tower under fire cannot recruit, so answer every red stream" (EN/AZ/RU/TR).
- AI (AI Engineer): `walkLandings` uses production 0 from the first hostile landing while landings are < `UNDER_FIRE_MS` apart (so `holdReserve`/`fallsAtMs` see the siege); personalities link when `spendable + genPerSecond(source) × SIEGE_PLAN_S ≥ costToTake + margin`, **`SIEGE_PLAN_S = 10`** (the trickle they are willing to wait for); the reference player's attack rule adds the same term; a sieged tower's response follows the order above (never counter-stream from the sieged tower without a surplus — that is the deadlock).

**Acceptance (before the marker above is removed).** Status 2026-09-19: 1–5 met (3e4a388: `tests/sim/underFire.test.ts` 1, 2a, 2b, 3a, 3b; `tests/ai/underFire.test.ts`; playtest 40/40 at 20 seeds), 6 met (3f48a1d re-clocked 9, 12, 19, 26, 27, 28, 31, 33, 34, 37, 38, 39). v2.1 is the shipped rule set; the "proposed" marker stays only as the date of the proposal.

2. Sim test: enemy L1 25 links into a player L3 100 over a 240 px road (a) unanswered: the player tower falls between 70 s and 90 s (v2: never, min 78); (b) a player L3 100 neighbour links into it at t = 5 s: it is back at 100 by t = 25 s and that supply line ends with `targetFull`.
3. Sim test: a tank factory trickle alone (one tank / 4 s) leaves the target regenerating between landings (`underFireUntilMs` expires); snapshots restore the field.
4. AI test: rusher L3 at 100 across from a player L3 at 100 (no other towers) links within 2 s of both reaching 100 (v2: never).
5. `npm run playtest -- --seeds 5`: no level × seed ends with outcome `playing` at 180 s (every run is a win or a real loss); level 40 seed 1 wins ≤ 120 s; levels 1–8 stay 100 %; the idle player still never wins.
6. Star clocks: the Level Designer re-measures medians after the change (expect −10…30 % on levels 9+) and re-derives `star3`/`star2` per the level-authoring skill.

## 2.0b Rules v3 (2026-09-21, stakeholder request) — "free lanes": supersede the road model of §2.0 / §2.1 / §2.3

**Stakeholder request (verbatim, AZ):** (1) "əgər qabağında maneə yoxdursa bütün qalalara hücum etmək olsun, xüsusi bir yol olmasın" — any tower may attack any tower that has no obstacle in front of it; there are no dedicated roads. (2) "yollar ancaq düz salına bilsin" — a path is always a straight line. (3) "bir qaladan digərinə əsgər göndərəndə göndərilən qalanın əsgər artırılması dayansın amma say dəyişməsin; qalanın levelindən asılı olaraq sürətlənsin əsgər getməsi" — while a tower sends, it stops growing but its number does not change; the sending gets faster with the tower's level.

**Rule set (authoritative; the paragraphs of §2.0 that talk about roads, draining, bridges, barriers and waypoints are superseded — auto-upgrade, the link limit, under fire, boosters, artillery, fortress and capture arithmetic stay).**

1. **Lanes instead of roads.** A level has towers and **obstacles**; it has no road list. Two towers are *connected* when the straight segment between their centres is **clear**: it crosses no obstacle and passes no *third* tower closer than `TOWER_BLOCK_RADIUS = 48 px` (a tower in the way is an obstacle — you cannot shoot through it). Every clear pair is a **lane**: straight, `points = [a, b]`, length = distance. Lanes are computed once in `createState` (they never change during a match — obstacles are static, towers do not move) and stored in `state.roads` under the existing `Road` shape (`kind` is always `'road'`, `cut` always false, `barrier` 0) so movement, clashes, arrivals, rendering and the AI keep their lane abstraction. Two lanes that *cross* each other geometrically do **not** interact: units clash only on the same lane in opposite directions (§2.3 unchanged).
2. **Obstacles** (`LevelDef.obstacles`, optional): `{ kind: 'wall' | 'water' | 'rock', points: {x,y}[], width?: number }` — a polyline of ≥ 2 points with a thickness (`width`, default `OBSTACLE_WIDTH = 28`); `rock` may be a single point (a disc of radius `width / 2`, default 40). A lane is blocked when the tower-to-tower segment comes within `width / 2` of any polyline segment (segment–segment distance) or of a rock centre. Kinds differ only in the drawing (stone wall, river, boulder); the sim treats them alike.
3. **Mines** (`LevelDef.mines`, optional): `{ x, y, charges }` points. At `createState` every lane records the mines whose centre lies within `MINE_RADIUS = 30 px` of the lane segment, as `(mineIndex, t)` with `t` the fraction along the lane from `road.a`. A unit that crosses `t` (same crossing test as the old road midpoint, direction aware) spends the mine exactly as §2.3: the first `charges` weight dies, a heavier unit survives and empties the mine; a spent mine is gone for every lane. Charges live in `state.mines[i].charges` (snapshotted). **Barriers and bridges are removed** (no `barrier`, no `kind: 'bridge'`, no `cutBridge` command, no `bridgeCut` / `roadCut`): a wall is a permanent obstacle, a river is a `water` obstacle with gaps where bridges used to be.
4. **Streams carry production, not garrison ("say dəyişmir").** A link is still `link { owner, from, to }` (valid when `owner` owns `from`, a lane joins them, `to ≠ from`, the link does not exist, and `from` has fewer than `LINKS_PER_LEVEL[level] = level` links). While a tower has ≥ 1 link its garrison **does not change by sending**: nothing leaves the garrison. Instead **each link emits its own stream**: the link accumulates `emitAccMs`; every `streamIntervalMs(from)` one unit spawns on the lane. `streamIntervalMs` **is the tower's production interval** for its kind and level, with the same multipliers as generation: barracks / fortress `GEN_MS[level]` = 1000 / 700 / 500 ms (1 / 1.43 / 2 units per s per link — this is "sürətlənsin, levelə görə"), artillery `× ARTILLERY_GEN_MUL` (2000 / 1400 / 1000), tank factory `TANK_GEN_MS[level]` = **4000 / 2800 / 2000** (a whole tank, weight 5, while the garrison holds ≥ 5 weight, else one infantry; `TANK_GEN_MS` becomes a per-level array for production too). The player's `productionMul` divides the interval, Overdrive multiplies the accumulation ×3, and **Freeze pauses the streams of every frozen owner** (the stream *is* production). Under fire pauses **growth only** — a tower under fire keeps streaming. Two links on an L2 each emit at the full L2 rate (streams are not shared); an L3 with three links puts 6 units/s on the map.
5. **Growth stops while streaming.** A tower with ≥ 1 link neither accumulates `genAccMs` nor auto-upgrades (§2.0 unchanged in effect: "inkişafı dayanır"). Friendly arrivals still reinforce it (+weight, capped) and still count toward the auto-upgrade *after* it is unlinked. Its garrison changes only by hostile landings (−), friendly landings (+), airstrikes (−) and capture.
6. **A stream needs soldiers.** The `link` command is ignored while `from.units < 1`, and a link emits only while `from.units ≥ 1` (a tank factory emits a tank while `≥ TANK_WEIGHT`, otherwise infantry while `≥ 1`). When a linked tower's garrison reaches 0 (it was shot down to 0 by hostile landings or an airstrike) **all its links end** with the new reason `sourceEmpty`, so it can grow again. Other auto-unlink reasons stay: `sourceLost` (capture) and `targetFull` (friendly target at capacity; for a supply line, at capacity and no longer relaying onward — rule 13). `roadCut` is removed.
7. **Straight only.** No waypoints: a unit walks the segment at `UNIT_SPEED` = 120 px/s (× `unitSpeedMul`, tanks × 0.7). Nothing else about movement, clashes (same lane, opposite directions, lighter dies), artillery (range 140 px, 1 kill / 0.8 s), fortress defence, capture (`damage > units` flips, remainder ≥ 1) or under fire (§2.0 v2.1) changes.
8. **Interaction.** Tap own tower = select; while selected, **every connected tower shows a thin guide line** from the selected tower (owner-coloured, 30 % alpha) so the player sees what is reachable. Tap a connected tower = link (tap again = unlink); tap a blocked tower = short shake + hint `hint.blocked` ("Yol bağlıdır" / "Blocked" / RU / TR); at the link limit the existing "L2 needed for 2 streams" hint. Drag tower → tower links too. **Swipe across an own stream = cut it (unlink)**: a drag that starts on empty ground or on any tower that is not the player's and whose path crosses one or more of the player's streams closes each of them (one `unlink` per stream crossed, at most once per swipe, live while the finger moves; a short slash trail follows the finger). A drag that starts on an own tower still means "link". *(BUG-33, 2026-10-11)* The long-press bridge cut is gone.
9. **Presentation.** Terrain draws no roads. Obstacles: `wall` = stone wall with battlement caps, `water` = river band with light ripples, `rock` = boulder; mines = the existing mine sprite at the point (spent → crater). Active links keep the owner-coloured chevron ribbon (straight). Sim events: `unitDied.cause` loses `'barrier'` and `'bridge'`; `bridgeCut` is removed.
10. **AI.** Bots and the reference player use the lane graph like the road graph (it is denser: most pairs are connected). Threat model under v3: a hostile stream into `T` lands `rate(from) = 1000 / streamIntervalMs(from)` units/s after the travel time and keeps `T` under fire, so `T` (which regenerates nothing while under fire) falls after `(T.units + 1) / Σ rates` seconds of landings — *the source's garrison no longer matters for the attack, only its level and how many links it can afford*. Sending costs nothing but growth: the trade-off the bots must weigh is **grow now (reach the next level = faster streams and one more link) vs attack now**. A counter-stream on the same lane cancels units 1:1 (equal levels: perfect stalemate, both frozen), so the answer to a siege is a **second** source or a reinforcement stream from a neighbour, and a tower at 0 must be unlinked/left to grow. Personalities: rusher links as soon as any target falls within `SIEGE_PLAN_S`; turtle grows to L2/L3 first and streams only from towers with a free link; opportunist targets towers already under fire from someone else. Exact constants: §2.5 to be rewritten by the AI Engineer.
11. **Levels.** Every level is re-authored on the new schema (`roads` removed — the validator rejects a level that still has one, `obstacles` / `mines` added). Conversion guide: a river with bridges becomes a `water` polyline with gaps at the former bridges; a barrier becomes a wall (permanent) or a mine (passable at a price); waypoints vanish; old roads that were the *only* intended paths become chokepoints by adding walls between the areas they separated. Bands (§3): obstacles appear from level 5 (lesson "walls block the line"), mines from 17 (unchanged), tower kinds unchanged. Star clocks re-measured with the reference player after the AI lands (`level-authoring` skill). Validator: every tower must be reachable from the player's first tower through lanes; every obstacle inside the map; no obstacle centre within `MIN_TOWER_DISTANCE / 2` of a tower centre.
12. **Save / replay.** `Tower` loses `linkCursor` and `drainAccMs`; `Link` gains `emitAccMs`; `GameState` gains `mines`; snapshot version bumps (continue/rewind include `emitAccMs` and mine charges). Old in-progress matches are not migrated (there are none persisted — saves hold progress, not matches).
13. **Relay through a full tower — supply lines (RELAY-1, 2026-10-12; "intentional only" by Producer decision the same day).** *Stakeholder report, TestFlight build 10, level 2 "Supply Line": "soldiers do not come out of the bottom tower" — home could not feed the front through a full mid tower.* Two cases, in plain words: **a drag or tap onto your own full tower that already streams onward = a supply line: it passes your soldiers on; reinforcing a tower until it is full still ends that stream by itself.** Exactly: (a) *Arrivals.* A unit of owner `O` that lands on a tower `T` owned by `O` with `T.units ≥ capacityOf(T)` (modified capacity for the player) while `T` has ≥ 1 outgoing link of `O` is **not absorbed** — whichever link brought it: in the same tick it leaves `T` again as a fresh unit on the next of `T`'s outgoing links, **round-robin in link creation order** (`Tower.relayCursor`, reset by a capture), same kind and weight (a clash-worn tank stays worn), normal speed (`UNIT_SPEED` × the owner's `unitSpeedMul`, tanks × 0.7), progress 0. `T`'s garrison, `genAccMs` and its links' `emitAccMs` are untouched — `T` keeps emitting its own stream, so a relay tower puts its production **plus** everything that arrives on the map. The arrival still emits `landed` (`hostile: false`). A full tower **without** an outgoing link absorbs as before (capped, overflow lost); a tower below capacity is reinforced as before; hostile landings are unchanged; the rule is owner-symmetric (bots relay too). (b) *Links.* At `link` time the link records `relay = true` when its target is the owner's, at or over capacity and **relays onward** (below); that is the supply-line gesture. A **relay link** ends with `targetFull` only when its target is full **and** no longer relays onward (re-checked at every prune: stop mid's onward stream and home → mid ends on that tick); if its target drops below capacity it simply absorbs and the link stays. A **plain link** into an own tower (made while the target was below capacity: a reinforcement) keeps the old rule — `targetFull` as soon as the target is full, even when the target streams onward — so the source is free to grow again and a player who only reinforces never builds a frozen chain; its last units still in flight are relayed on arrival rather than capped away. *Relays onward* means: `T` has an outgoing link of `O` that leads, directly or through further full `O` towers that relay, to a tower that takes the unit (not `O`'s, or below capacity) — a **closed loop of full towers takes nothing**, so relay links around it end with `targetFull` instead of circulating units for ever. Input: a tap / drag from an own tower into an own full tower that relays onward is a plain `link` command (the sim marks it a relay; the BUG-35 `hint.full` refusal is gone); a full tower at its top level that does not relay keeps the BUG-38 refusal `hint.fullTop`. Snapshot version 3 (`Tower.relayCursor`, `Link.relay`). Tests: `tests/sim/relay.test.ts`.

**Structural consequence (measured 2026-09-21 by the AI Engineer, binding for level design).** Equal streams on one lane cancel exactly and a shielding tower never comes under fire, so a tower whose defender shields every lane it can be attacked from is untakeable by equal-level streams: **an enemy tower is only takeable when the attacker can bring more lanes to it than the defender has links (L1 = 1, L2 = 2, L3 = 3; a fortress 2)** — or a faster stream (higher level) on one lane. Level rule: every enemy or neutral tower that the player must take needs **≥ 4 clear lanes** (≥ links + 1 for the level it will reach; the validator warns below 4 from band 2 on), and walled keeps get their gaps on **more** sides, not fewer. Bots with `aggression < DEFENCE_MIN_AGGRESSION = 0.35` never shield/counter/reinforce (tutorial bands). The design pillar behind it: under v3 the player wins by *surrounding* and by *growing a spare tower to the next level* while the front is frozen — not by piling garrison.

**Constants added / changed (`src/sim/constants.ts`):** `TOWER_BLOCK_RADIUS = 48`, `OBSTACLE_WIDTH = 28`, `ROCK_RADIUS = 40`, `MINE_RADIUS = 30`, `TANK_GEN_MS = [0, 4000, 2800, 2000]`; removed: `LEAVE_INTERVAL_MS`, `LONG_PRESS_MS` (input), road kinds. Unchanged: `GEN_MS`, `CAPACITY`, `LINKS_PER_LEVEL`, `UNIT_SPEED`, `UNDER_FIRE_MS`, artillery, fortress, boosters.

**Arithmetic (for the Level Designer and the AI).** One L1 stream (1/s, 240 px lane = 2 s travel) flips a neutral 10 at ≈ 13 s, an enemy L1 at 25 at ≈ 28 s (under fire from the first landing, so no regen); an L3 (2/s) does it in ≈ 15 s; three L3 links on one target (6/s) flip an L3 keep at 100 in ≈ 19 s. Growing: an idle L1 reaches 25 (→ L2) in 25 s from 0, L2 reaches 50 in 17.5 s more, L3 100 in 25 s more. So "grow to L2 then attack" pays off when the target holds more than ≈ 25 units or when a second link is worth it. Equal streams on one lane cancel exactly; a level difference nets `rate(high) − rate(low)` per second on the weaker side.

**Acceptance (Producer gate before the report).**
1. Sim tests: (a) two towers, wall between → `link` ignored; wall removed → lane exists, straight, length = distance. (b) A third tower on the segment (within 48 px) blocks the lane; at 49 px it does not. (c) L1 25 links into neutral 10 over 240 px: source stays at 25 the whole time, target flips between 12 and 14 s; L3 100 into enemy L3 100: flips between 50 and 54 s with the source still at 100. (d) Two L1 25 streaming at each other over one lane: no landing ever, both stay at 25, no capture within 120 s. (e) A linked tower shot to 0 loses its links with `sourceEmpty` and grows again on the next tick without fire. (f) Mines: a mine 20 px off a lane kills the first `charges` weight crossing; 40 px off does nothing. (g) Freeze pauses enemy streams; overdrive triples the player's. (h) Snapshot round trip keeps `emitAccMs` and mine charges; determinism test still passes.
2. `npm run playtest -- --seeds 3`: 50/50 levels won by the reference player within 180 s; daily / weekly / twist gates green; the idle player never wins level 1.
3. e2e: smoke (tap → guide lines → link → win level 1), a blocked tap shows the hint, obstacles visible in the level screenshots; bundle ≤ 82 kB gzip (80 until the 2026-10-12 visual pass, then 88 / 92; PERF-9 on 2026-10-14 moved the perspective camera and the enemy AI into lazy chunks: 88.4 → 79.7 kB).
4. GDD §2.1 / §2.3 / §2.5 / §3 rewritten to v3; level lessons for 1–5 and every level that taught bridges / barriers rewritten in EN / AZ / RU / TR.

**Designer's read, 2026-09-22.** v3 turns the player's loop from "how many do I send" into "which towers do I let grow": every tower presents one choice at a time — *grow* (idle: an L1 reaches L2 in 25 s, which buys the second link and the 1.43/s rate) or *stream* (send now at the rate you already have, number frozen) — and because equal streams cancel 1:1 on a lane and a shielded tower never comes under fire, a front tower that streams is a wall, not a spear. The win therefore comes from **surrounding** (more lanes into the target than it has links — the structural consequence above) and from a **spare tower growing** behind the frozen front until it out-rates or out-links the defender; that is pillar 2's tension in v3 form (the number you watch is no longer "how much is left" but "who reaches the next level first"). Two known weaknesses. (1) *Equal-lane stalemate*: when the lane count into a keep equals its link count and both sides sit at the same level, nothing moves — ribbons cross, nobody is under fire, nobody grows; the §3 "≥ 4 lanes" rule keeps it out of shipped levels, but a human can build it by hand on any chokepoint map by shielding every lane. (2) *Lean twist stalemate at L3*: once an enemy reaches L3 (2/s per link, three links) while the player runs `productionMul` 0.9 (L3 = 1.8/s), the player can never hold a lane 1:1 — every shield leaks 0.2/s — so the player must always out-lane, and on a keep with exactly four lanes that single spare link is one the enemy's own shields can cancel; level 48's L3 start is a level patch for this, not a rule. Levers if telemetry shows players stuck (attempts per level > 4, or matches with no capture in their last 60 s): (a) **defender-only cancelling** — the later-opened stream on a lane is the *shield* and cancels the earlier one 1:1 for at most `SHIELD_MS` = 8 s from its own start; after that the lane stops cancelling and every unit of both streams lands, so a shield buys time but never wins by itself; (b) *attacker wins ties* — on an exactly equal lane the earlier stream's units land at a small surplus; (c) *link limit L1 = 2* (`LINKS_PER_LEVEL` = [0, 2, 2, 3]). Recommendation: **(a) with `SHIELD_MS` = 8 s — proposal, not scheduled.** (b) breaks pillar 1 (a cancelled count that is not the sent count is a hidden rule) and makes the first mover win every mirror, which the AI would exploit before any human; (c) rewrites the tutorial band and its first lesson ("grow to L2 for the second stream" loses its reason) and doubles the tutorial enemies' pressure; (a) keeps every number visible (the ribbon that has crossed for 8 s starts landing, the badge goes alert — the player sees exactly when a shield stops being a plan), leaves every level's opening untouched, turns both stalemates into "the side that keeps attacking wins in finite time" with the level difference or the extra lane deciding, and is one timer per lane in the clash step plus the same timer in the AI's `laneFlow` / shield rule (the turtle loses its wall, so it must re-plan as a counter or a reinforcement) — one sim test (two L1 25 streaming at each other: no landing before 8 s, the earlier stream landing after) and a full playtest before it is scheduled.

### 2.1 Map
- A level is a set of **towers** with `x,y` in a 720×1280 logical space, plus static **obstacles** and **mines** (§2.0b rules 2–3). There is no road list. *(v2, superseded by §2.0b: roads (edges, optionally with `waypoints`) were the only paths.)*
- Two towers are **connected** when the straight segment between their centres is clear: it comes no closer than `width / 2` to any obstacle (segment–segment distance, or distance to a rock's centre) and no third tower's centre lies closer than `TOWER_BLOCK_RADIUS` = **48 px** to it (strictly less blocks; 49 px is clear — §2.0b acceptance 1b). Every clear pair is a **lane**: straight, `points = [a, b]`, length = distance. Lanes are computed once in `createState` and never change during a match (obstacles are static, towers do not move). Units travel only along lanes; two lanes that cross geometrically do not interact.
- Obstacles: `{ kind: "wall" | "water" | "rock", points, width? }` — a polyline of ≥ 2 points with thickness `width` (default `OBSTACLE_WIDTH` = **28**); a `rock` may be a single point, a disc of radius `width / 2` (default `ROCK_RADIUS` = **40**, i.e. width 80). The kinds differ only in drawing; all block alike, permanently.
- Mines: `{ x, y, charges }` points. Each lane records every mine whose centre lies within `MINE_RADIUS` = **30 px** (inclusive) of its segment, as the fraction `t` along the lane from `a`; a mine can sit on several lanes at once (§2.3).
- Owners: `neutral`, `player`, `enemy1`, `enemy2`, `enemy3`. Colours: grey, blue, red, green, yellow.
- Validator (`scripts/lib/validateLevel.ts`, the sim's own `laneClear`): every tower reachable from the player's first tower through lanes; a `roads` key fails the level; every enemy or neutral tower the player must take should have ≥ 4 clear lanes from band 2 on (warning below 4 — §2.0b structural consequence, §3).

### 2.2 Towers
| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `kind` | `barracks` (default), `artillery`, `tankFactory`, `fortress` |
| `owner` | see above |
| `units` | integer garrison, ≥ 0 |
| `level` | 1..3 |

- Generation (owned, non-neutral): barracks L1/L2/L3 produce 1 unit every **1.0 / 0.7 / 0.5 s** (`GEN_MS`) up to capacity **25 / 50 / 100** (`CAPACITY`, §2.0 ladder; every kind uses it). Neutral towers never generate. A tower generates nothing while it is **streaming** (≥ 1 outgoing stream, §2.0b rule 5), under fire (§2.0 v2.1) or frozen (§2.6). *(v1, superseded by §2.0: capacity 30 / 50 / 80.)*
- Upgrade (§2.0): **automatic and free** — a L1 or L2 tower whose garrison reaches its capacity becomes L+1 on the spot, garrison kept; a tower with ≥ 1 outgoing stream never upgrades — it accumulates no production while it sends, and friendly arrivals still add to it and count toward the upgrade once it is unlinked (§2.0b rule 5) *(v2, superseded by §2.0b: "it drains instead")*. L3 is the top; the garrison caps at 100 and production stops adding. *(v1, superseded by §2.0: tap the selected tower to pay 10 / 20 units; the `upgrade` command is now a no-op kept for replays.)*
- Capture keeps the tower's level and clears its under-fire window; the old owner's outgoing streams end (`sourceLost`) and the new owner may re-link; the new owner's modifiers (§2.7) apply from that tick.
- `fortress`: capacity ×1.5 (**37 / 75**), and hostile weight is halved through a defence accumulator (**2 attackers remove 1 defender**; a 5-weight tank removes 2.5, the half carries over). Max **L2**, so at most 2 streams.
- `artillery`: generates at half rate (interval ×2: 2.0 / 1.4 / 1.0 s) and each of its streams emits at that same interval (§2.0b rule 4); every **0.8 s** kills 1 hostile unit within **140 px** — a lane death, not a landing, so it never puts the target under fire. Capacity: the ladder (25 / 50 / 100). *(v1: capacity 40.)*
- `tankFactory`: produces a **tank** (weight 5, speed ×0.7) every **4.0 / 2.8 / 2.0 s** by level (`TANK_GEN_MS`) instead of infantry, and each of its streams emits at that same interval; capacity counted in weight on the same ladder (25 / 50 / 100, so 5 / 10 / 20 tanks); a stream sends a whole tank while ≥ 5 weight is garrisoned, otherwise one infantry (while ≥ 1). *(v1: max 40. v2, superseded by §2.0b: one tank every 4 s at every level.)*

### 2.3 Sending units (streams — §2.0b rules 4–8 are authoritative for the link rules)
- Interaction (§2.0b rule 8; `src/input/pointer.ts`): tap own tower = select (highlight, and every connected tower shows a thin guide line from it in the owner's colour, 30 % alpha); tap a connected tower = open a **stream** to it (tap again = close it; drag tower → tower also opens one — a drag onto an existing stream is a no-op, a drag from an own tower never closes); swipe across an own stream = cut it (unlink) — a drag starting on empty ground or a non-player tower closes every player stream its path crosses *(BUG-33, 2026-10-11)*; tap a tower with no clear lane = short shake + `hint.blocked` ("Blocked — no clear line"). A tower runs at most `level` streams (L1 1, L2 2, L3 3; fortress max L2 = 2); the tap beyond that is refused with a shake and `hint.linkLimit` "L{n} needed for {n} streams". A stream cannot be opened from a tower holding < 1 unit (the command is ignored, no hint). Tap the selected tower again or empty ground = deselect. *(v1, superseded by §2.0: one tap sent all garrisoned units, a "SEND 100 % / 50 %" toggle halved it, and tapping the selected tower upgraded it. There is no send-ratio toggle and no manual upgrade. v2, superseded by §2.0b: a 500 ms long-press cut a bridge; there is no long-press.)*
- A stream **carries production, not garrison** ("say dəyişmir"): the source's number does not change by sending. Each stream of a tower emits one unit every `streamIntervalMs(from)` = the source's own production interval for its kind and level — barracks / fortress **1.0 / 0.7 / 0.5 s**, artillery **2.0 / 1.4 / 1.0 s**, tank factory **4.0 / 2.8 / 2.0 s** — divided by the player's `productionMul`, accumulating ×3 under Overdrive and paused by Freeze. Streams are independent: an L2 with two streams puts 2 × 1.43 units/s on the map, an L3 with three 6/s. A stream emits only while the source holds ≥ 1 unit (a tank factory emits a tank while ≥ 5 weight, else one infantry). The unit walks its straight lane at **120 px/s** (`UNIT_SPEED` × `unitSpeedMul`; tanks × 0.7). Each unit has `weight` (infantry 1, tank 5). The stream keeps flowing until closed (`manual`), the source changes owner (`sourceLost`), the source is shot to 0 by hostile landings or an airstrike (`sourceEmpty` — every stream of that tower ends and it grows again), or the friendly target is full (`targetFull`) — except a **supply line**: a stream opened into an own full tower that already streams onward passes the soldiers on and ends only when that tower is full and no longer streams onward (§2.0b rule 13, RELAY-1 2026-10-12); reinforcing a tower until it is full still ends that stream by itself. A stream into a neutral or hostile tower never ends by itself. *(v2, superseded by §2.0b: the source drained one unit every 0.12 s (`LEAVE_INTERVAL_MS`) round-robin over its streams, and a cut road ended the stream (`roadCut`).)*
- Arrival at friendly tower: `units += weight` (capped at capacity; overflow lost); the arrival counts toward the auto-upgrade when the tower is not streaming. Exception (§2.0b rule 13, RELAY-1): at a **full** friendly tower with ≥ 1 outgoing stream the unit is not absorbed but leaves again at once on the tower's streams, round-robin, same kind and weight; the garrison does not change.
- Arrival at hostile tower: `units -= weight` (fortress: `weight/2` via the accumulator) and the tower is under fire for 1.5 s (§2.0 v2.1) whether or not it flips. If damage exceeds the garrison (strictly greater) the tower flips: `owner = attacker`, `units = remaining weight` (fortress remainder = `weight − 2×defenders`), minimum 1. Equal damage leaves the tower at 0 under the old owner.
- Two units of different owners on the **same lane** walking toward each other: when they cross, the lighter dies and the heavier loses that weight (equal weights: both die; `unitDied.cause = 'clash'`). Two equal streams facing each other on one lane therefore cancel exactly and nothing lands on either end. Units on different lanes never meet, even where the lanes cross on screen.
- Mine (a **point** hazard, §2.0b rule 3): a unit that crosses the mine's fraction `t` on its lane this tick (direction aware, the same test on every lane the mine touches) is hit: if the mine's remaining `charges` ≥ the unit's weight, the unit dies (`unitDied.cause = 'mine'`) and `charges −= weight`; a heavier unit survives intact and sets `charges` to 0. A spent mine is gone for every lane at once (`state.mines[i].charges`, snapshotted). *(v2, superseded by §2.0b: a mine sat at a road's midpoint and belonged to that road.)*
- **Barriers and bridges do not exist** under v3 (§2.0b rule 3): no `barrier`, no `kind: "bridge"`, no `cutBridge` command, no `roadCut` / `bridgeCut` events; `unitDied.cause` ∈ { `clash`, `artillery`, `mine` }. A wall is a permanent obstacle (§2.1); a river is a `water` obstacle with fords (gaps) where bridges used to be. *(v2, superseded by §2.0b: a barrier at a road's midpoint was worn down by the weight of units walking into it; a bridge road could be cut by its endpoint's owner, killing the units on it.)*

### 2.4 Win / lose
- Win: no tower is owned by any enemy and no enemy unit is walking on any lane (`getOutcome`, checked once per tick after arrivals). *(v2, superseded by §2.0b: "legacy send queues included" — the one-shot `sendUnits` command is accepted and ignored under v3, so there is no queue to count.)*
- Lose: player owns no tower and no player unit is walking on any lane.
- Stars: 3 if time ≤ `level.star3`, 2 if ≤ `level.star2`, else 1 (`starsFor`). Time is **sim time**: pause and speed ×2 do not change it; a Continue (ECONOMY.md §3.5) rewinds the sim 20 s but keeps the original clock. Clocks are derived per level from the reference player's median (`level-authoring` skill); the current values are in `src/levels/manifest.ts` (15–50 s for 3★).

### 2.5 AI (rules v3 free-lane model, 2026-09-21, revised 2026-09-22 for AI-6 / AI-7, 2026-09-23 for AI-8 and 2026-09-24 for AI-9; `src/ai/`; every bot ticks every `AI_TICK_MS` = 0.5 s)
Everything below reads only what a human sees on screen: garrisons, levels, kinds, owners, lanes, mines, ribbons (links, with their age), units on lanes and the booster effects. No bot issues `sendUnits` or `upgrade`. Bots are pure functions `(state, owner, rng) => Command[]`; the rng is used only by the enemies' aggression gate. Enemies also fight each other. Code: `common.ts` (threat model), `tactics.ts` (shared rules), `personalities.ts`, `referencePlayer.ts`.

**Threat model** (`common.ts`; all rates in weight per second, tanks count 5).
| Field | Meaning | Numbers |

**Shared rules** (`tactics.ts`; one `Ctx` per owner per tick: links issued this tick count for later rules, each tower starts at most one new stream per tick — readable ribbons).
- **maintain** (per linked tower): (a) *upgrade break* — a linked tower at capacity below its top level cannot auto-upgrade: it drops every link for one AI tick when it survives ≥ `UPGRADE_BREAK_SAFE_MS` = **3 s** without them, takes the level (the sim upgrades an unlinked tower at capacity on its next 50 ms tick, under fire or not), and re-links next tick at the better rate (an L1 at 25 shielding an L1 becomes an L2 that out-rates it). The tower is **marked used for the rest of the tick**: AI-6 (thinWalls, an L2 home starting at 40/40) was the attack rule re-linking it to another neighbour in the same tick, so the sim never saw it unlinked — the test `tests/ai/upgradeBreak.test.ts` proves L3 and a new stream within 2 AI ticks for the reference player and the rusher; (b) a supply line into an own tower ends unless it is a *reinforcement* (the target falls without it, or a hostile stream / column still heads for it) or the personality keeps it (below); (c) a stream into a contested neutral ends when the race is lost — `contestOf` gives the flip to a rival and we do not take it back within `contestMarginMs` (the enemies: 0, pure parity; the reference player: `CONTEST_MARGIN_MS`) — it only feeds their capture; (d) a stream that lands less than `MIN_LANDING_SHARE` = **25 %** of what it emits ends (an L2 stream alone into a gun post under lean lands 3 % — it cost the home its growth for 40 s on level 14), unless it *shields* — a hostile stream runs the other way on the same lane: the two cancel 1:1, and a **partial** shield (ours slower) is kept too, since only their surplus lands and a tower under fire grows nothing anyway (dropping an out-rated shield the moment the enemy reached L2 let its whole stream land and lost level 13 under thinWalls / lean); (e) an attack whose target no longer falls within `HOPELESS_MS` = **60 s** ends once it has run `MIN_ATTACK_MS` = **5 s** (a landing attack is committed for five seconds, so the two sides do not link and unlink every tick); (f) *retreat* — a tower under fire below `RETREAT_UNITS` = **3** drops every stream that is not winning its lane (shield, or landing on a target that falls within `HOPELESS_MS`), so it can grow again when the fire stops.
- **defend** (per own tower that falls to what is coming): (1) *counter* — besiege every attacker's **source** from other towers within `COUNTER_PLAN_MS` = **30 s** (a linked source does not grow, so it falls at `(units + 1) / rate` after the travel time, and its capture ends the stream — the answer GDD §2.0b rule 10 asks for); (2) *reinforce* — when the tower would still fall before the counter frees it (`fallsAt ≤ counterDone + RACE_MARGIN_MS`), `reinforcePlan` from friendly neighbours with a free link; (3) *shield* — stream back on the lane of the strongest attacker whose rate the tower matches (never when outrated: that stream would only be cancelled), reclaiming a supply line that is no reinforcement or an attack whose target outlives the tower — and, with `reclaimReinforcement` (the reference player only, AI-7b), **its own reinforcement line when the shield makes the tower hold**: the tower's capture would end that line anyway (`sourceLost`), so a last tower whose only link feeds a neighbour saves itself first. Off for the enemies: switched on for them it made the opportunist on level 14 and the turtle on 42 hold their keeps 20 s / 5 s longer (30 seeds), which is a balance change the Level Designer did not ask for. Equal streams on a lane are a standoff, so a shield is the last resort, not the plan.
- **attack** (`siegePlan` per target, best score first, repeated while sources remain): targets are the hostile towers adjacent to any own tower with a free link (or a link it may reclaim); a target already falling within the horizon to our own streams is left alone (no piling); for a neutral, `flipOwner` with the plan must be us (a contested neutral is a race) and, with `holdCheck`, the strongest rival neighbour with a free link must not out-rate what our adjacent towers could pour into it after the capture. **Anticipation** (`anticipate`): the plan is judged against the defender's shields — the target is assumed to stream back on the lane of every source it can match, one per free link, strongest first — so an L2 target with two free links is not attacked by two L2 streams (both would be cancelled) but by three. (Until 2026-09-22 every friendly neighbour with a free link was also assumed to reinforce the target; with the corrected artillery model that made the bot too timid — under lean it never opened on level 13 and lost 5/5 while the turtle grew to L3 — and the enemies' own defence *counters* our sources before it reinforces, so the assumption was mostly false.) A source without a free slot may give up a supply line that is no reinforcement, or (`races`) a **shield whose tower outlives the new target by `RACE_MARGIN_MS` = 1 s** (the enemy cannot shield a lane while its links are busy, so the race is worth it — the way out of the level-3 four-tower standoff).
- **stack** (`stack`, 2026-09-22; reference player and turtle): an *idle* own tower — a free link and nothing of its own to take once `attack` has run — joins a siege already running (a hostile tower with our stream into it, existing or issued this tick) when its stream lands ≥ `MIN_LANDING_SHARE`, brings the fall forward by ≥ `STACK_MIN_GAIN_MS` = **0.5 s** without a shield on its lane, and is not slower under the defender's anticipated shield on that lane (the defender has only so many shields: moving one onto the new lane frees another). Under v3 a stream costs nothing but the source's growth, so a source left idle beside a siege only delays it — the minimal fastest-first `siegePlan` on level 1 left the camp idle while the home alone took 3 s longer. The reference player's *grower* stays out, except when the enemies are down to their last tower (there is nothing left to grow for — level 1). Not for the rusher and the opportunist: with it they pile every tower onto one target (level 8, rusher at a = 0.3: the reference player fell from 20/20 to 14/20), which is not the per-tower rusher the design asks for. Measured (50 levels × 5 seeds): 30 levels faster (11: 46.6 → 35.6 s, 28: 65.7 → 49.5, 32: 67.2 → 51.4, 36: 64.8 → 45.4, 45: 65.0 → 49.6, 47: 60.8 → 46.3), campaign total −210 s; slower at 20 seeds only on 39 (88.5 → 92.7) and 41 (74.3 → 77.6).
- **supply**: a capped keep (top level at capacity — it makes nothing, streaming costs it nothing) with a free link pours into the friendly neighbour nearest the front that is not full (the sim ends the line at `targetFull`).

**Personalities** (`personalities.ts`; aggression `a` in 0..1 per enemy in the level JSON). Every own tower's decisions are skipped this tick with probability `(1 − a) × 0.5` (one rng draw per tower, level order). Plan horizon `planMs = SIEGE_PLAN_S × (0.5 + 0.5 a)`. Below **`DEFENCE_MIN_AGGRESSION` = 0.35** an enemy does not defend at all (no counter, reinforcement or shield — a tutorial enemy attacks and lets its towers be taken); from there the defence above applies in full. Tick order: gate → maintain → defend → attack (→ supply).
- All three share `maintain` / `defend` / `attack` / `supply` (and so AI-6 and the AI-7c artillery model), but not the reference player's *grow first* (the turtle has `TURTLE_MIN_LEVEL`) and not `reclaimReinforcement`.
- `rusher` — `SIEGE_PLAN_S` = **30**: from any tower, any level, links the hostile neighbour that falls soonest within the horizon; one ribbon per tower whatever its level (`RUSHER_MAX_LINKS` = 1); plans against what is on the map now (no anticipation: it opens, and drops the stream after 5 s if it turned out hopeless); drops a shield for a race it wins. At a = 1 an L1 at 8 over 600 px attacks a player L1 at 18 (falls at 30 s) but not at 19; at a = 0.2 (18 s horizon) not even a 10 (22 s).
- `turtle` — `SIEGE_PLAN_S` = **45**: opens no attack from a tower below `TURTLE_MIN_LEVEL` = **2** (its towers grow first: 25 s from 0 to L2, 42.5 s to L3), then uses every link its level allows (`TURTLE_MAX_LINKS` = 3), plans against the defence (anticipation), keeps its shields, and its capped keeps supply the neighbour nearest the front.
- `opportunist` — `SIEGE_PLAN_S` = **45**, up to two ribbons per tower (`OPPORTUNIST_MAX_LINKS` = 2, the second from L2): scores a target already under fire from someone else (a rival's ribbon into it, or under fire without ours) as if it fell 60 s sooner, so it joins sieges; races like the rusher.
- Enemy supply lines into a captured tower end once it holds `SUPPLY_FULL_UNITS` = **5** unless the source is a capped keep.

**Reference player** (`referencePlayer.ts`; the bot `npm run playtest` uses — every level must be winnable by it within 180 s, §3; deterministic, the rng is unused and ties break by tower id). Rules in priority order each tick: **maintain → defend → capture → attack → supply** (the shared rules with `HOPELESS_MS` = 60 s, `COUNTER_PLAN_MS` = 30 s), with:
- *capture* — `CAPTURE_PLAN_MS` = **30 s**, neutrals only, `holdCheck` on; a contested neutral is taken when `contestHeld` says it ends up ours — we land the flipping unit, or take it back from the rival's garrison of 1 within `CONTEST_MARGIN_MS` = **0.5 s** (a unit already on the lane: level 26's tank 52 ms behind red's 4th infantry) — and is scored by *our* acquisition time (our flip or our retake), not by the fall the rival's stream helps bring about, **plus its hold cost** (`holdCost`, `holdMs` above, AI-9 — the rival's landings before the flip still count for us; the enemies pay no hold cost, as they never paid the flat penalty); cheap L1 streams first: a captured tower streams at full rate from its first unit. (AI-8c, 2026-09-23: until then a contested neutral scored a flat `CONTEST_PENALTY_MS` = 10 s worse, which ceded every cheap contested neutral to any free one within 10 s. The margin is small on purpose: a retake that depends on *later* landings gives the rival time to shield or reinforce — at 2 s it cost lean 14 36.6 → 51.0 s, lean 23 44 → 36/50, lean 49 50 → 47/50, plain 14 +7.6 s and 43 +4.7 s; 0 and 0.5 s measure alike everywhere but the tank case.)
- *attack* — `ATTACK_PLAN_MS` = **45 s**, enemy towers only, anticipation on, focus fire on the tower that falls soonest with every adjacent tower stacked until it breaks (`siegePlan`), reclaiming supply lines and racing shields.
- *defend* — as the shared rule, with `reclaimReinforcement` on (AI-7b) and *finish the capture* (`finishMs` = `FINISH_MS` = **3 s**, AI-8b): a shield never reclaims an attack whose target falls within 3 s unless the tower being defended falls before that capture completes *even with the shield in its place* — both computed with the threat model; a shield that comes too late costs the capture and saves nothing (the trace fixture: home at 1 under two columns, the fort one landing from falling — the 2026-09-22 bot dropped the fort stream for a shield on one lane while the other column took home anyway). Off for the enemies.
- *grow home* (`growHome`, `GROW_HOME_MS` = **8 s**, AI-8a; runs after defend, before capture) — the tower within 8 s of its next level at its own growth rate (below its top, not under fire or siege, not used by this tick's defence) whose streams are all *dispensable* — a supply line that is no reinforcement, or a siege our other streams still carry alone (the target still falls within `HOPELESS_MS` to them and a contested neutral stays ours); never a shield, never a capture only it carries — drops them (rule `grow`) and is reserved from capture, attack and stack until it has the level; one tower at a time, an unlinked one already growing preferred (sticky), then the grower order. Under lean an L2 home that streams from the first tick never reaches L3 (its garrison changes only by landings, and every rule re-links it the moment it has a free slot): this is the lever that lets it out-level a frozen front.
- *stack* — after attack, before supply (rule 3b above); *grow first* applies only against ≥ `GROW_FIRST_MIN_ENEMY_TOWERS` = **2** enemy towers (on a one-enemy-tower map attacking now is always faster).
- *grower* — the highest own tower below its top level, rearmost (most hops from an enemy), then fullest, that is not linked or under fire, is kept out of captures while we hold ≥ `GROWER_MIN_TOWERS` = **2** towers, and joins attacks only as the last source when the others cannot break the target — one keep reaches L2/L3 (faster streams, more links, better shields). Measured (levels 1–50 × seed 1): with and without the grower the campaign differs by ±1 level; the rule is kept because the design asks for it.
- *grow first* (`growsFirst`, AI-7a) — a **lone** home (fewer than `GROWER_MIN_TOWERS` towers) that reaches its next level within `GROW_FIRST_MS` = **8 s** at its own growth rate opens no capture or attack until it has the level: L2 doubles its links and speeds every stream, so the first capture lands sooner anyway and a second runs beside it. It still maintains and defends, and never waits while linked, under fire or under siege. Measured (50 levels × 5 seeds): 8 s speeds up levels 6, 7 and 41 by 10–12 s and slows none; 10 s costs level 4 1.6 s; 15 s slows a dozen levels by 8–26 s (1, 2, 10, 13, 18, 33–35) — the wait only pays when it is short. An L1/20 home on level 12 (scratch copy) now wins 10/10 seeds, so a level ≥ 9 no longer needs an L2 start when the home is within 8 s of L2.
- A tower with no takeable target stays unlinked and grows.

**What the bot does about the lean stalemate (AI-7d, structural — for the Level Designer).** Under `lean` (×0.9 production) a player stream never out-rates an equal-level enemy stream on one lane, so once an enemy barracks is L3 and shields every lane the front is frozen for good; the same holds on plain levels for any keep with ≥ links lanes (§2.0b "Structural consequence"). The bot's answers, in the order it reaches them: (1) **surround** — `siegePlan` stacks one source per lane until the keep has more hostile lanes than links (`anticipate` assumes it shields the strongest `maxLinksOf` lanes), so a keep with ≥ 4 clear lanes always falls to enough L2/L3 neighbours; (2) **out-level** — the *grower* keeps one rear tower unlinked so it reaches L3 while the front is frozen, then joins as the last source (an L3 stream nets `2 − 1.43` = 0.57/s on an L2 lane, and 2 v 2 under lean is still 1.8 v 2 — lost, so under lean the surplus must come from a *level* difference, not the multiplier); (3) **starve the source** — *counter* besieges the attacker's linked source (it does not grow while linked); (4) **race** — a shield is dropped for an attack whose target falls first (`RACE_MARGIN_MS`), because the enemy cannot re-shield a lane while its links are busy. What it cannot do: break an L3 keep whose lane count ≤ 3 with equal levels, or beat a shielded lane under lean by rate alone — those levels pass only through a fortress / artillery keep (level limit 2 links, or a post the player takes first), an early win before the enemy reaches L3, or ≥ 4 lanes. Level rule stays: every keep the player must take needs **≥ links + 1 clear lanes** at the level it will reach; under lean the Level Designer should also not put the player's only lane-count advantage behind an L3 barracks.

**What the model says about rules v3 (for the Level Designer).** Two equal streams on one lane cancel exactly, so an enemy tower whose lane count to our towers is ≤ its link count (L3: 3; fortress L2: 2) and that streams back on every lane is **untakeable** by equal-level attackers — level 7's `base` (one lane, via `fwd`), 15's `citadel` and 16's `keep` (two lanes, fortress L2), 8's `base` (two lanes, L3) end in a full-map standoff at 100 v 100 whatever the bot does. Every enemy keep needs more attack lanes than links, or a weaker level than what the player can reach, or an enemy below `DEFENCE_MIN_AGGRESSION`. A 1-lane chain (level 2: home—mid—foe) is a standoff from the moment the enemy holds `mid`. Contested neutrals are a parity race (`flipOwner`): who links first does not decide, the count of landings does.

### 2.6 Boosters (meta, paid in gold)
- **Overdrive**: ×3 production for 10 s (**30 gold**) — under v3 every stream of the caster accumulates ×3 too, since the stream *is* production (§2.0b rule 4).
- **Freeze**: every owner except the caster stops generating **and streaming** for 5 s (**40 gold**); frozen units already on a lane keep walking.
- **Airstrike**: remove 10 units from one enemy tower (**50 gold**); a streaming tower brought to 0 loses every stream (`sourceEmpty`, §2.0b rule 6) and starts growing again.
A pre-paid **charge** (booster crate, rewarded ad) is consumed before gold; the gold price takes the commander discount (§2.7) and the premium discount, together capped at −30 %, rounded up. Boosters are unavailable in the Daily Challenge (§7.2). *(v1: "coins" — the currency is **gold** since save schema v3, 2026-09-13.)*
Gold: **10 per star on the first clear**, 10 per newly earned star on a replay, 3 per star on any other replay (≤ 100 gold a day); the full earn/sink model and the second currency (crystals: skins, skip, continue, crate) live in `docs/ECONOMY.md` §2–§3. *(v1: "10 per star, first-clear only".)*

### 2.7 Player modifiers (commander upgrades and daily twists)
The sim carries `state.modifiers: PlayerModifiers` for the **player** owner only (enemies always run the defaults); a tower or unit uses the modifiers of its *current* owner, so a captured tower switches ladders the moment it flips. Four axes, validated in `createState`:
| Field | Effect in the sim | Campaign ceiling (`ADVANTAGE_LIMIT`, ECONOMY.md §3.2) | Daily twist value (§7.2) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `productionMul` | generation interval **and stream interval** ÷ value (§2.0b rule 4; stacks with Overdrive) | 1.06 | 0.9 (Lean) |
| `capacityMul` | capacity × value, floored, min 1 — auto-upgrade and `targetFull` use the modified capacity | 1.25 | 0.8 (Thin walls) |
| `startGarrisonBonus` | + units on every player tower at start, capped at capacity | +2 | +5 (Reinforced) |
| `unitSpeedMul` | unit speed on the lane × value, applied when the stream emits the unit | 1.04 | 1.25 (Fast feet) |
Commander upgrades are bought with gold in the shop (five tracks, 1 100 gold each; the fifth track is a booster discount and touches no sim number). Design rule: with every track maxed the reference player's median over 40 levels × 20 seeds must stay ≤ 2.5★ (`npm run playtest -- --upgrades max --seeds 20`, currently 2.0★); if it fails, the tier effects shrink, never the prices. In a Daily Challenge `state.modifiers` is exactly the twist, whatever the save holds.

### Authoring for the first-time line (rules v3, 2026-09-24, GD-1 — apply, do not re-derive)
The tutorial band is built for a player who taps once and watches. Every number below is the code's (`scripts/lib/naivePlayer.ts`, `src/ai/personalities.ts`, `src/ai/tactics.ts attack`, `src/ai/common.ts siegeOf`, `src/sim/constants.ts`); check a layout with the arithmetic first and with `npm run playtest -- --naive --seeds 5` second.

**1. The naive line, exactly.** First action at **3.0 s**, then every **4 s** (`--react 8000`: 8 s), on the AI tick. Each own tower in id order with a free link (L1 = 1, L2 = 2, L3 = 3) opens **one** stream to the nearest connected tower it does not own — **neutral before enemy, then lane length, then id**. It never unlinks (a stream ends only by the sim: `targetFull` at 25 / 50 / 100, `sourceEmpty` at 0, `sourceLost` on capture), never boosts, never stacks on purpose, and re-links a tower that lost every stream one full reaction later. It draws no rng: the seed moves only the enemy's aggression gate. Consequences: the home is frozen on its first target from 3 s until that target is *full* (a neutral of N units at d px flips at `4 + d/120 + N` s and fills about 25 − garrison s later — level 2: `side` at 9.3 s, `home` free at 32.3 s); every captured tower links to *its* nearest on the next reaction, so all player towers sit at L1 until a `targetFull` frees one; the first landing of the first tap is at `4 + d/120` s (level 2: 6.3 s).

**2. What a rusher does at t = 0.** From every tower with ≥ 1 unit it links the connected non-own tower that **falls soonest within `planMs = SIEGE_PLAN_S[rusher] × (0.5 + 0.5 a)` = 30 × (0.5 + 0.5 a) s**: a = 0.1 → **16.5 s**, 0.2 → **18**, 0.3 → **19.5**, 0.4 → **21**, 0.5 → **22.5**. A neutral of N units at d px from an L1 barracks falls at **`1 + d/120 + N` s** (one interval before the first unit, the walk at 120 px/s, then N + 1 landings at 1/s; the threat model returns exactly this — level 2 `mid` 6 u at 480 px = 11.0 s, level 8 `mid` 14 u at 407 px = 18.4 s, outside 18); a *growing* player L1 tower falls at `2 × (1 + d/120) + N` (it grows until the first landing: level 1 `home` 18.6 s). The only randomness is the gate — each tower's decision is skipped with probability (1 − a) × 0.5 per 0.5 s tick — so an opening comes at 0 s or a few ticks later. A target already falling within the horizon to its own streams is skipped (no piling; the rusher has no `stack` rule), so **k rusher towers open on k different targets: a single bait holds exactly one of them** (the GD-1 finding). The turtle opens nothing below L2 (its first stream at 25 − N s at the earliest, then 1.43/s); below a = 0.35 no enemy defends.

**3. Three patterns that hold** (levers: +1 unit = +1 s of fall time, +120 px = +1 s, −0.1 aggression = −1.5 s of horizon).
- **Nothing inside the horizon at t = 0.** Every target a rusher tower can see falls later than `planMs`: the tower stays idle and grows instead (level 8 `base` → `mid` 20.0 s > 18; level 3 `foe` → `home` 29 s). List the lanes of every enemy tower and put the player's first two targets on the far side of the number.
- **Tower in the way.** A lane is blocked when a third tower's centre lies within **48 px** of the segment (`TOWER_BLOCK_RADIUS`), a wall or river within **14 px** (width 28 / 2), a rock within **40 px**. Put the player's first target behind something from *every* rusher tower: level 2 `mid` sits on the `foe`–`home` line; 5's wall hides `home`; 7's rocks at (464, 560) / (256, 560) hide `se` / `sw` from `base`; 8's walls at y = 700 hide `nW` / `nE` from `foeW` / `foeE` / `base` and both homes from `foeW` / `foeE` (`base` → homes is a lane, 979 px = 34.2 s, outside the horizon) — but not the cross lanes `foeW`–`nE` (595 px, 12.0 s inside 18), which is LV-15.
- **Home pocket.** No enemy tower has a lane onto the home at t = 0 (blocked, as in 2, 5, 7, or beyond the horizon, as in 3 and 8): nothing lands on it, so it is never under fire, and the moment its first stream ends (`targetFull`) it grows to L2 on its own in 25 − units s (level 6: `home` free at 32.6 s, L2 at 34.6 s while the turtle's `base` reached L2 at 19.0 s — the line still wins at 96 s). A **bait** (level 2: `mid` falls at 11.0 s, `side` at 11.2 s) works only against one rusher tower and by a strict margin; prefer the three above.

**4. Anti-patterns (BUG-13, 2026-09-23).** (i) *Shared nearest neutral* — the naive first target is also a rusher's soonest target. A contested neutral at equal rates goes by **parity, not by who is first**: the flipping landing is the (N + 1)-th; when the second stream is less than 1 s behind the landings alternate, so N odd → the *second* lander wins, N even → the first — and the gate decides who is first, so the outcome is a coin flip per seed. Level 3 passes 4/5 only this way (`east`, 5 u: seed 2 the rusher lands first at 6.3 s and the player takes the 6th landing at 9.1 s; seed 1 the rusher is gated to 1.0 s, lands second, flips at 9.3 s) — do not copy it. Once lost, the loser's 1/s lands hostile on a tower the winner's 1/s refills: 0/1 forever (old 2). (ii) *Single-lane chain* — a home whose only lane leads to the contested tower is frozen for the match (old 2, old 8 `homeW`). (iii) *All own towers linked at L1* — the line freezes every capture at 1/s; a turtle at L2 by 19–30 s out-rates each lane at 1.43/s and takes them back one by one (old 6). Give the line a `targetFull` before the turtle's L2 (small neutrals near home) or a pocket that grows.

**5. Gate and clock.** `npm run playtest -- --naive --seeds 5` and `--react 8000` (the script exits 0 — read the table): levels **1–8 ≥ 4/5 at 4 s and ≥ 3/5 at 8 s; 9–16 ≥ 3/5 at 4 s** (2026-09-24: 1–16 all pass; 3 is 4/5 at both delays; 13 and 15 are 0/5 at 8 s, which the 9–16 gate does not ask). `scripts/reclock.ts` floors **star2 on 1–16 at the 4 s naive median rounded up to 5 s** (capped 180 s; 1–8 since 2026-09-23, 9–16 since 2026-09-27 — item 10, `NAIVE_FLOOR_LAST_LEVEL` = 16) so a first-time player gets 2★ on every naive-gated level (2: 72.0 → 75 s, 6: 96.0 → 100 s; 9–16 in item 10); the 8 s medians are not floored (2, 5, 7, 8 exceed star2 at 8 s — accepted; 13 and 15 do not hold at 8 s at all, which the 9–16 gate does not ask).

**6. **7. The ≥ 4-lane rule in the tutorial band (LV-16, decided 2026-09-25 — ratified: level 8 `foeW` / `foeE` stay at 3 lanes).** The §2.0b structural consequence exists only because a defender *shields*: equal streams cancel on a lane and a shielded tower never comes under fire, so the attacker must out-lane the defender's links. Shielding is gated by `DEFENCE_MIN_AGGRESSION = 0.35` (`src/ai/personalities.ts`: the shared tick runs `defend` — reinforce, counter, shield — only when the enemy's `aggression ≥ 0.35`, for **every** personality, rusher, turtle and opportunist alike; below it an enemy only attacks). Therefore the **≥ 4 clear lanes** rule binds every enemy or neutral tower on a level with **any** enemy at aggression ≥ 0.35 (a neutral can be captured and then shielded by that enemy), and does **not** bind a level whose every enemy is below 0.35: there the lane count only limits how many streams the *player* can bring, and one L1 stream flips an unshielded L1 keep (level 8 `foeW` 4 u: `base`, `foeE`, `mid` are its three lanes; from `mid` alone it falls in ≈ 1 + 460/120 + 4 ≈ 9 s). Such a keep still needs **≥ 2 lanes** (one lane is the single-lane chain of anti-pattern 4.ii). Band 1 (levels 1–8) must keep every enemy **below 0.35** (JSON 2026-09-25: 0.1–0.3) — that is what makes the validator's exemption by level id (`LANE_WARNING_FROM_LEVEL = 9`, `scripts/lib/validateLevel.ts`) correct; a tutorial level that ever raises an enemy to ≥ 0.35 is bound by the 4-lane rule by hand, since the validator does not warn there. ## 7. Daily Challenge (v1, 2026-09-18)

Code: `tower-clash/src/daily/challenge.ts` (pure picker, no `Date`), tests `tower-clash/tests/daily/challenge.test.ts`. UI, save and result handling: Frontend Engineer, from this section. Backlog item DAILY-1.

### 7.1 Purpose
A retention hook in the Tower War "daily map" mould: one fixed match per day that every player in the world plays with the same level, the same seed and the same handicap, so "did you beat today's?" is a real question and the answer is comparable. It reuses only vocabulary the game already has (a level from the campaign, the player-side modifiers the sim already supports for commander upgrades) — no new mechanic, no new tower, no new hazard. It also gives a player who has finished the campaign a reason to come back for 2–3 minutes a day (pillar 4), and it is deterministic, so the reference bot can prove every day's map is winnable before the day arrives.

### 7.2 Rules

**Day key.** The challenge is keyed by the UTC calendar day, `dayKey = YYYY-MM-DD` of `new Date()` in UTC (`dayKeyOf`). One challenge per key. Time zone is UTC **by design** (one map for everyone, one reset for everyone); the card says so (§7.3).

**Picker (`challengeFor(dayKey)` — cycle version, DAILY-4, 2026-09-20).** `FNV(s)` = FNV-1a-32 over the string `s` (offset `0x811c9dc5`, prime `0x01000193`, `Math.imul`, unsigned); `n` = the day number, whole days since 2026-01-01 UTC (`dayNumberOf`: 2026-01-01 → 0, negative before it); `P` = pool size = **42**:
| Field | Rule | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Pool | the levels with id 9 … 50 in manifest order — **42** levels (`POOL_FROM` 9, `POOL_TO` 50; 9–40 = 32 levels until 2026-09-20) | tutorial band 1–8 excluded: no lesson, no handicap on a lesson |
| Cycle | `cycle = floor(n / P)`, `pos = n mod P` (non-negative) | 42-day cycles anchored on 2026-01-01: cycle 6 = 2026-09-10 … 2026-10-21, cycle 7 starts 2026-10-22, cycle 8 on 2026-12-03 |
| Order | `order = seededOrder(pool, FNV("cycle#" + cycle))` — Fisher–Yates over a copy of the pool, swaps from the end, random steps from mulberry32 seeded by the hash | one fixed permutation per cycle; every pool level appears **exactly once per cycle** |
| `levelId` | `order[pos]` | never the same level twice inside a cycle; adding or removing an id in 9–50 changes `P` and re-maps every future day; changing a level's content does not |
| `seed` | `(FNV(dayKey) mod 1 000 000) + 1` | unchanged from v1: fixed for the day, same enemy decisions for everyone (§2.5 bots are deterministic per seed) |
| `twist` | `twistForDay(n)` (DAILY-5, 2026-09-27). `raw(k) = TWISTS[FNV(dayKey(k) + "#twist") mod 5]` is the v1 draw (a second hash so the twist does not correlate with the level or the seed). **Even** `n` → `raw(n)`. **Odd** `n` → `raw(n)` unless it equals `raw(n−1)` or `raw(n+1)`; then `allowed[FNV(dayKey + "#twist2") mod |allowed|]`, `allowed` = the 3–4 twists that are neither neighbour's raw draw, in `TWISTS` order | **never the same twist on consecutive days.** Proof: two consecutive days are one even day (plays its raw draw) and one odd day (avoids both neighbours' raw draws), so they differ. O(1) and never recursive: an odd day reads *raw* draws only, never another day's effective twist, so no chain runs back through the calendar. Distribution stays uniform by symmetry (the banned set is a rotation-invariant draw, the fallback uniform over the rest): over 2026-01-01 … 2027-12-31 (730 days) each twist is 17.9–22.1 % (plain 134, lean 161, fastFeet 131, thinWalls 147, reinforced 157) and 116 days (15.9 %) leave their raw draw. Unit test: no two consecutive days share a twist over those 730 days, every twist within 15–25 % |
| Malformed key | throws | the UI never passes anything but `dayKeyOf(now)` |

Worked example (pin it in tests, exact values): `2026-09-21` (n = 263, cycle 6, pos 11) → level **44**, seed **44606**, **Lean rations**; `2026-09-22` → level **31**, seed **711749**, **Reinforced**; `2026-09-23` → level **21**, seed **489368**, **Thin walls**. Twist-guarantee example (DAILY-5): `2026-09-26` (n = 268, even) → level **50**, seed **601273**, **Reinforced** — its raw draw; `2026-09-27` (n = 269, odd) draws Reinforced too, so it plays `allowed` = [Classic, Lean rations, Fast feet, Thin walls] at `FNV("2026-09-27#twist2") mod 4` → level **49**, seed **378892**, **Fast feet** (the first shipped re-mapped day; Reinforced until 2026-09-27). Over the 60 days from 2026-09-18 the picker visits 38 distinct levels and every twist 7–15 times (plain 7, lean 14, fastFeet 15, thinWalls 11, reinforced 13); over October 2026 23 distinct levels and all 5 twists; over 400 days never the same level on consecutive days (unit test: every cycle from 2026-01-01 visits all 42 levels, any 14-day window inside a cycle has 14 distinct levels) and over 730 days never the same twist on consecutive days. *(The v1 pins `2026-09-18` → 18 / 233226 / Plain, `2026-09-19` → 31 / 455607 / Fast feet, `2026-09-20` → 19 / 266987 / Fast feet are retired: the level id re-mapped with DAILY-4, the seeds are unchanged, and DAILY-5 moved `2026-09-19` (odd) from Fast feet to **Lean rations** because `2026-09-20`'s raw draw is Fast feet as well.)* First shipped weekly, `2026-09-21` → level 33, seed 743047, Fast feet, target 0:35 (§8; 0:40 before the rules v3 re-clock).

**Twists** — exactly one per day, applied as the **player's** `PlayerModifiers` (§2.0 implementation: enemies always run the defaults; a captured tower takes its new owner's ladder the moment it flips):
| id | Display name | Modifier | What the player sees |
|---|---|---|---|
| `plain` | Classic (EN; draft name "Plain") | none (`DEFAULT_MODIFIERS`) | the campaign level as shipped, upgrades off |
| `lean` | Lean rations (EN; draft name "Lean") | `productionMul` 0.9 | player interval ÷ 0.9: barracks L1/L2/L3 = 1.11 / 0.78 / 0.56 s (0.90 / 1.29 / 1.80 units/s); artillery half of that; tank factory one tank / 4.44 s. (0.85 in the first draft: the 60-day sweep lost 15 of 60 lean runs, 0.9 loses 8 — see §7.5) |
| `fastFeet` | Fast feet | `unitSpeedMul` 1.25 | player infantry 150 px/s, tanks 105 px/s (applied at spawn; a 240 px road takes 1.6 s instead of 2.0 s). The only twist that is a pure buff |
| `thinWalls` | Thin walls | `capacityMul` 0.8 | player ladder 20 / 40 / 80 (fortress 29 / 60; artillery and tank factory 20 / 40 / 80). Towers auto-upgrade at the smaller capacity, so L2 and L3 come sooner, but a finished keep holds 80 and a supply line ends (`targetFull`) at 80 |
| `reinforced` | Reinforced | `startGarrisonBonus` 5 | +5 units on every player-owned tower at match start, capped at that tower's capacity |

Rationale: the five twists are the four modifier axes the sim already validates in `createState` plus the unmodified case; two are handicaps (Lean, Thin walls), two are buffs (Fast feet, Reinforced), so the daily is sometimes harder and sometimes easier than the campaign clear. Numbers are chosen so the reference player still wins the whole pool under each (§3, §7.5); anything stronger than ×0.9 / ×0.8 failed levels in the 33–40 band in the 60-day sweep and in earlier commander-upgrade sweeps.

**Equal for everyone.** In a daily match `state.modifiers` is **exactly** the twist's modifiers: commander upgrades (ECONOMY.md §3.2) are not applied whatever the save holds; the booster bar is hidden and booster commands are ignored; "Reinforcements" continue (ECONOMY.md §3.5, rewind + Freeze + 15 infantry) is not offered; the level skip is not offered; the rewarded "×2 gold" is not offered on the daily result. Speed ×2 and pause are allowed (the star clock is sim time). Skins and terrain themes apply (cosmetic). Tutorial overlays never show.

**Unlock.** The card is locked until `save.stars['8'] ≥ 1` (level 8 cleared with any stars). Locked card text: "Clear level 8 to unlock". Rationale: by level 8 the player knows links, under fire and the star clock; the pool starts at 9.

**Locked levels.** The daily may pick a level the player has not reached. It is playable as the daily anyway and **writes nothing into campaign progress**: no `stars[levelId]`, no level unlock, no first-clear gold, no milestone / band / achievement, no `defeats` counter, no drill pay. The campaign is the campaign; the daily is a separate ledger (`save.challenge`).

**Rewards — first win of the day only.**
| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Gold | `30 + 10 × stars`, stars clamped to 0…3 → a win pays **40 / 50 / 60** for 1★ / 2★ / 3★ (`goldReward`) |
| Crystals | **5** |
| Replays the same day | 0 gold, 0 crystals; the local best may improve |
| Loss | nothing; **unlimited free retries**, the reward stays available until 00:00 UTC |
| Streak milestone (implemented 2026-09-18, ECONOMY.md §6.2) | **+5 / +20 / +100** crystals, once, when the first win of the day brings the streak to day **3 / 7 / 30** (`STREAK_MILESTONES`); a broken streak forgets the paid days (`save.challenge.milestones`), so a rebuilt run earns them again; nothing retroactive; the result line shows the crystal total and a toast names the bonus |
Stars use the level's own `star3` / `star2` clocks unchanged (§2.4); a twist may make 3★ easier or harder that day, that is the point — a Fast feet day on a star-clock boundary level (12, 14, 28, 40 per ECONOMY.md §6.1) is a near-certain 3★ and pays 60, accepted. **Attempts:** unlimited, free, all day (Monetization asked for a ruling): a one-attempt rule would make a 2-minute level a coin flip and punish exactly the returning player the feature exists for; the loss writes nothing, not even a defeat counter. The `rv_daily_retry` idea (a second attempt with a free Freeze) only makes sense under a one-attempt rule and breaks "equal for everyone", so it is **not** in v1 (§7.6). `rv_double_gold` is not offered on the daily result (ECONOMY.md §6.1 agrees). Yearly ceiling for a player who wins every day at 3★: 21 900 gold + 1 825 crystals — comparable to the login streak (ECONOMY.md §2.1: 330 gold + 20 crystals per week), intentionally the larger of the two because it costs a real match.

**Streak.** `save.challenge.streak` counts consecutive UTC days with a first win. On the first win for day `D`: `streak = (lastWinDay == D − 1 day) ? streak + 1 : 1`, `lastWinDay = D`. Displayed value: `min(streak, 99)`, and **0** when `lastWinDay` is neither today nor yesterday (the streak is broken; the stored number is simply overwritten by the next win). It is independent of the login-reward streak (`save.daily`, local calendar day, ECONOMY.md §2.1) — two counters, two rules, never merged in v1.

**Day 30 — decided 2026-09-20 (closes the §7.6 open question).** The day-30 bonus **pays once per streak run, as shipped**; there is **no repeating "every 30 days" bonus** (no day 60 / 90 tier) in v1. Reasoning: (a) the milestone is a *moment* — "you stayed a month, here is the $0.99 pack" — and moments repeat badly: the second 100 is bookkeeping, not a reward, and the pill would have to advertise "+100 at day 60" for a player who no longer needs a hook; (b) the player who would earn a repeat is the one with a 30-day-plus streak — under the 70 % model ≈ 0.02 % of active days, but in practice the most engaged and most likely paying group, and a repeating 100 (≈ +3.3 crystals a day on top of the daily's 5) turns a retention lever into a subsidy for exactly the people it does not need to move; (c) the daily already pays 1 825 crystals a year at 3★, the largest earn source in ECONOMY.md §2.1, so any repeat lands on the "raise the cheapest skins" side of §6.1's sink check; (d) a broken streak forgets the paid days, so the "earn it again" path exists — it costs a fresh month, which is the point. UI stays as shipped: past day 30 the pill reads "Streak N" with no next-bonus text. **Revisit** with 30 days of streak data after 1.0: if ≥ 5 % of weekly-active players reach day 30, add *distinct* tiers (day 60 → 50, day 100 → 100, each once per run) rather than a repeat; if < 5 %, leave it.

**Local best.** `save.challenge.best[dayKey] = { stars, timeMs }`, updated when a result is better (more stars, then lower time). Only the **30 most recent day keys** are kept (`CHALLENGE_BEST_KEEP`, pruned on write and on normalize; keys sort chronologically as strings) so the save stays bounded; there is no history screen in v1 (§7.6). "Done today" = `lastWinDay == today` or `today in best`.

**Save schema.** `SaveData.challenge: { lastWinDay: string | null; streak: number; best: Record<string, { stars: number; timeMs: number }>; milestones: number[] }`, added without a schema bump (as `achievements` was). `milestones` = the streak-milestone days (3 / 7 / 30) already paid in the current run; cleared when a first win starts a new run (`streak` back to 1). Normalize: `lastWinDay` must match `^\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}$` or become `null`; `streak` a non-negative integer (stored uncapped, shown capped at 99); `best` keys validated the same way, `stars` clamped to 0…3, `timeMs` a non-negative integer, entries beyond the 30 newest dropped; `milestones` distinct non-negative integers. Code: `src/ui/save.ts` (`ChallengeState`), rules in `src/ui/daily.ts` (`recordChallengeResult`, `shownStreak`, `challengeDone`, `challengeUnlocked`, `msToUtcMidnight`), constants in `src/daily/challenge.ts` (`REWARD`, `STREAK_MILESTONES`, `UNLOCK_AFTER_LEVEL`).

### 7.4 Edge cases
- **Day rolls over mid-match.** The `dayKey` captured when the match started is the one the result is booked against: the win counts for the day it was **started** (pays that day's reward if not yet claimed, updates that day's best, sets `lastWinDay` to that day). The card afterwards already shows the new day, not done. A win for `D` recorded on `D + 1` still lets `D + 1` extend the streak (`lastWinDay == D`).
- **Time zone.** UTC by design; the card shows the countdown to the reset, so a player in Baku sees it land at 04:00 local. *(Deviation, 2026-09-19: the shipped card does not print "UTC" anywhere — `daily.newIn` / `daily.newInTime` are a bare countdown. Design asks Frontend to add "(00:00 UTC)" to the countdown pill's tooltip line or the locked-state line 3 when it fits at 360 px; the countdown alone is acceptable for v1.)* The login streak stays on the local calendar day — documented asymmetry, revisit in v2.
- **Local clock.** There is no server; the device clock is trusted. Setting the clock forward yields at most one 60-gold + 5-crystal reward per faked day, less than one rewarded ad — accepted for v1, noted for the leaderboard (v2).
- **Level not unlocked.** Playable as the daily; does not unlock it or anything else (§7.2 "Locked levels").
- **Level not loadable** (manifest missing the id, network failure on the lazy level chunk): the card shows the error toast the campaign uses and stays tappable; nothing is written.
- **Pool edits.** Adding or removing an id in 9–50 changes the pool size `P`, so the cycle length, the per-cycle order and every future day re-map (the 2026-09-20 flip 9–40 → 9–50 did exactly this, in a numbered release). Do it only with a note in the daily report, never mid-day, and re-pin §7.2.
- **Same level as the campaign level in progress.** Allowed; the two runs are separate matches and separate ledgers.
- **Replay after the reward.** Free, unlimited, pays nothing, can improve the local best; the result line says "claimed today".
- **RETRY / restart after the rollover** (BUG-9, 2026-09-19). The result screen's RETRY and the pause menu's restart re-read the day key: when the challenge's day has passed they do not replay it (a win would book against yesterday) — the level map opens with the toast "New daily challenge is ready" (`daily.newReady`) and the player starts today's from the card. Campaign retries are unaffected.

## 8. Weekly Challenge (WEEKLY-1 — picker implemented 2026-09-20 in `src/daily/challenge.ts` (`weeklyFor`, `weekKeyOf`, `isMondayKey`, commit 6399883, with unit tests); UI, save and playtest mode in progress)

The next retention feature after the daily: a longer, harder fixed match that lasts a week, so a player who cannot beat it on Monday has six more evenings, and a 3★ target that turns "I won" into "I can do it faster". Same principle as §7: everyone in the world plays the same level, seed and twist; the reference bot proves the week before it starts. No new mechanic.

### 8.1 Rules
- **Week key.** `weekKey` = the `dayKey` (§7.2) of the week's **Monday in UTC** (`weekKeyOf(date)`: `dayKeyOf(date − ((utcDay + 6) mod 7) days)`); the week runs Monday 00:00 UTC to the next Monday 00:00 UTC. Example: 2026-09-20 (a Sunday) → `2026-09-14`; the first shipped week would be `2026-09-21`. A key that is not a Monday throws (the UI only ever passes `weekKeyOf(now)`).
- **Picker** `weeklyFor(weekKey)` — pure, no `Date`: pool = levels **33 … `POOL_TO`** = 33 … 50, **18** levels (`WEEKLY_POOL_FROM` = 33; bands 4 and 5, since `POOL_TO` went to 50 on 2026-09-20); `h = FNV-1a-32(weekKey + "#week")`, `levelId = pool[h mod pool.length]`, `seed = (h mod 1 000 000) + 1`; twist = `WEEKLY_TWISTS[FNV-1a-32(weekKey + "#weektwist") mod 4]` where `WEEKLY_TWISTS` = the four **non-plain** twists (Lean rations / Fast feet / Thin walls / Reinforced — a weekly is always twisted, the campaign clear is the daily's job); **3★ target** = the level's own `star3` clock, unchanged.
- **Equal for everyone** — identical to §7.2: `state.modifiers` = the twist; no upgrades, no boosters, no Reinforcements, no skip, no ×2 gold; speed ×2 and pause allowed; skins apply.
- **Unlock.** `save.stars['32'] ≥ 1` (every vocabulary item has been taught by 32; the pool starts at 33). Locked text: "Clear level 32 to unlock". A pool level the player has not reached is playable as the weekly and writes nothing into campaign progress (§7.2 "Locked levels" rule).
- **Rewards, per week key.** First win at any stars: **100 gold** once. Reaching the 3★ target (sim time ≤ `star3`) at any time during the week, first or later attempt: **20 crystals** once. Both can land on the same run. Replays are free and unlimited, pay nothing further and may improve the local best; a loss writes nothing. Yearly ceiling 5 200 gold + 1 040 crystals; the 20 crystals are the same as the day-7 daily milestone and pay for a *3★ on a band-4/5 level under a twist*, the hardest routine thing in the game. Monetization sizes: ≈ +2.9 crystals a day for a player who hits every target — flag for §6.1's 14–15 a day band; if it overshoots, the target part drops to 15, never the gold.
- **Streak of weeks.** `save.weekly.streak`: on the first win for week `W`, `streak = (lastWinWeek == W − 7 days) ? streak + 1 : 1`; shown as 0 when `lastWinWeek` is neither this week nor last week; capped at 99 for display. Week-streak milestones: next item. Independent of both daily counters.
- **Week-streak milestones (decided 2026-10-06, Game Designer — POST_LAUNCH.md §6 roadmap row, pulled forward as the stakeholder's 2026-10-05 default).** `WEEKLY_STREAK_MILESTONES = [[4, 15], [8, 30], [12, 60]]` (`[weeks, crystals]`, exported from `src/daily/challenge.ts` next to `STREAK_MILESTONES`), paid once per streak run:

  | First win of the week brings `save.weekly.streak` to exactly | Crystals | Per run, cumulative |
  |---|---|---|
  | **4** (a month) | **15** | 15 |
  | **8** (two months) | **30** | 45 |
  | **12** (a quarter) | **60** | 105 |

  - **When paid.** By `recordWeeklyResult` (`src/ui/weekly.ts`), only in the first-win branch (`w.lastWinWeek !== weekKey`) and after the streak update: `m = WEEKLY_STREAK_MILESTONES.find(([weeks]) => weeks === w.streak)` on the **raw stored** streak (never the display value capped at `STREAK_SHOWN_MAX` = 99, which is unchanged); if `m`, `earnCrystals(save, m[1])`. Exactly 4 / 8 / 12: 5–7, 9–11 and everything past 12 pay nothing — **no 16, no repeat every 4 weeks in v1** (same reasoning as the daily's day-30 ruling, §7.2). Replays in a week already won, losses and the 3★ target pay no milestone; the target is independent, so one run can pay 100 gold + 20 (target) + the milestone. There is no weekly practice run (Yesterday's map, §7.3, is daily-only and never reads or writes `save.weekly`).
  - **What breaks the streak.** A week key with no win: the next first win sets `streak = 1` (§8.1 streak rule above), and the run's paid milestones are forgotten, so a rebuilt run pays 15 / 30 / 60 again at its own weeks 4 / 8 / 12. Nothing retroactive: a save that already holds `streak ≥ 4` when this ships pays only when a later first win lands exactly on 8 or 12.
  - **No new save field.** `streak` only moves by +1 (first win in the week after `lastWinWeek`) or back to 1, so inside one run each value is reached exactly once — the value is the ledger, and "forgets the paid milestones" is automatic. `save.weekly` stays `{ lastWinWeek, streak, best }` (no `milestones` list; the daily's `save.challenge.milestones` is redundant for the same reason and stays as shipped). A clock moved backwards books a first win at `streak = 1` (its `lastWinWeek` is not the previous week), so it cannot repeat a milestone; a faked forward clock earns at most 105 per 12 faked wins — accepted, as "device clock trusted" below. A hand-edited `streak` is normalized to a non-negative integer as today and pays only when a later first win lands on 4 / 8 / 12 — accepted (local save).
  - **`WeeklyOutcome`** (return value only, not the save) gains `milestone: number` (0 or the bonus paid); `crystals` becomes everything this call paid = (20 if `targetHit`) + `milestone`, as `DailyOutcome.crystals`; the `weekly.resultTarget` toast therefore reads `WEEKLY_REWARD.crystals`, not `crystals`.
  - **`weekly_streak_4` stays and stacks.** The achievement (ECONOMY.md §6.3: `save.weekly.streak >= 4`, 20 crystals, once per save, evaluated after every weekly result) is unchanged and fires on the same result as the first week-4 milestone: the first run's week 4 pays **15 + 20 = 35** crystals (plus 100 gold, plus 20 if the 3★ target falls on that run); every later run's week 4 pays 15. Reason: they are different things — the achievement is a one-off catalog badge (ceiling 145, pinned by tests), the milestone a per-run moment a rebuilt streak earns again; folding one into the other would either make the second run's week 4 worth more than the first or remove a shipped catalog row. The achievement's 20 rides on the achievement grant, never in `WeeklyOutcome.crystals` (§6.3 call-site rule).
  - **Result text.** `weekly.resultMilestone` (EN) = **"Week streak {n} · +{crystals} crystals"** → "Week streak 4 · +15 crystals" (`streak` = `WeeklyOutcome.streak`, `crystals` = `WeeklyOutcome.milestone`; AZ / RU / TR in the same commit). Shown on the result screen in the achievement-toast slot (3.5 s), as `daily.milestone` (§7.3), with the same priority rule: achievement unlocked on the same result > week-streak milestone > weekly 3★ target (`weekly.resultTarget`). One toast per result; a lower-priority bonus is credited, not named (on the first run's week 4 the toast is the `weekly_streak_4` achievement, whose name already says "4 weeks in a row"). The line under the stars stays `weekly.resultWon` ("Weekly challenge done · +100 gold · streak 4"). The week-streak pill stays "Week streak N" with **no next-bonus text** in v1 (§7.3, §8.2 unchanged).
  - **Rationale.** Pulled forward without data because the roadmap's trigger (weekly participation, reviews) cannot be observed before launch, the cost is bounded (≤ 105 crystals per run and 0 recurring once a run passes week 12), and the week streak was the only counter in the game with nothing on it. 4 / 8 / 12 are a month, two months and a quarter — the calendar a once-a-week player thinks in — and 12 equals the `WEEKLY_BEST_KEEP` history. Sizes sit under the daily ladder per calendar day (105 / 84 days ≈ 1.25 a day against 125 / 30 ≈ 4.2, since a week costs one win, not seven) but above it per win (≈ 8.8 against ≈ 4.2, a band-4/5 level under a twist), with week 4's 15 below the weekly 3★ target's 20 so the target stays the weekly's headline crystal, and week 12's 60 below day 30's 100; ECONOMY.md §6.1 band: ≤ +1.25 a day for a player who wins every week, for 12 weeks, and ≈ 0 for the first-time line (four consecutive weekly wins at 40 % ≈ 2.6 % of four-week windows).
- **Save.** `SaveData.weekly: { lastWinWeek: string | null; streak: number; best: Record<weekKey, { stars: number; timeMs: number; target: boolean }> }` — `target` = the 20 crystals were paid; keep the **12** newest week keys (`WEEKLY_BEST_KEEP`); normalize as `challenge` (§7.2), keys must be Mondays. No schema bump.
- **Edge cases** — as §7.4 with "week" for "day": the `weekKey` captured at match start is the one booked; RETRY / restart after the Monday rollover re-reads `weekKeyOf(now)` and returns to the map with "New weekly challenge is ready" (`weekly.newReady`); device clock trusted (one faked week = 100 gold + 20 crystals, accepted); a pool edit re-maps every future week — do it with a note, never mid-week; the same level may be today's daily and this week's weekly (separate ledgers).
