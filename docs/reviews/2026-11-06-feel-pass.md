### Phase notes (first person, measured)

- **Responsiveness.** I press home on level 1: the half-alpha ring is there within 52 ms, before I lift. I drag to camp and let go: 74 ms later the ribbon is drawn and the `send` blip plays (+78 ms). A shop tap is slower: the button loses its 2 px shadow while held, then the shop appears **141–166 ms after release** with a hard cut (`shop-open-360x640-0296ms.png` still title at +64 ms after release, `-0577ms.png` shop) — sluggish for a menu, outside the core loop.
- **Impact.** Capture: halo + flash ring + chime on one frame, chips by +117 ms, settled ≈ +500 ms — crunchy (light tier). Lost tower: crack flash 250 ms + thud at +1 ms + jolt — readable, no longer hollow. Booster cast: sound at +4 ms, but the button itself shows nothing until the world updates (between +39 and +125 ms frames), and active Overdrive turns its icon grey inside a gold ring (`zoom-L15-overdrive-bar-360.png`), the same grey a disabled button uses. Airstrike: hollow (Finding 2).
- **Rhythm.** Stream → muster → march → landing ticks (varied, F7) → capture now chains without a gap. Over a 30–90 s match the bed is the same Am–F–C–G 9.6 s loop (≈ 3–9 repeats) with the pulse layer simply on while play frames come (`music.ts` `tick`: `want = recent && !ended`); a home tower dropping ×15 → ×11 under L15's assault changes only the knock rate — monotonous.
- **Clarity.** Numbers at 360 hold (badges, `→1` chips, `×` under-fire pip). The L1 coach mark is the one cryptic moment (Finding 1). Airstrike's number jumps with no count-down.
- **Payoff.** The win sequence is now staged: undimmed beat, card, a chord per earned star, coins into the wallet, toast last. The paid actions are uneven: Freeze / Overdrive show for their whole duration, the airstrike is over in ~100 ms.
- **Dead time.** None unintentional in the loop. Result: the card reads "+0" from +832 to ≈ +1 510 ms while stars land — authored anticipation, keep.

### Forcing questions

- **Q1, sound only (music off).** I hear: a blip per stream, varied arrival taps, a wooden knock when they hit me, a low thud when I lose a tower, a chime when I take one, the mine boom, the gun's thump, glass for Freeze, a brass swell for Overdrive, star chords and coin ticks on the card. I **cannot** tell: that I just won (the same chime as any capture until +1.2 s); that an airstrike landed rather than the enemy gun (same `artillery` voices); how close I am to losing (the bed does not change; with music on only the knocks tell). **Mostly pass** (was a fail).
- **Q2, first action.** Press → ring ≤ 52 ms (snappy). Release → ribbon 74–91 ms (responsive, sim tick). Release → a soldier on screen at once (muster), the sim's first unit at +1 067 ms.
- **Q3, 20th repetition.** Captures and arrival taps hold (variation, capped chips). What wears first now is the music loop (one 9.6 s phrase, no development) and the identical `send` blip on every re-link.
- **Q4, no VFX.** Freeze / Overdrive keep their badge washes; a lost tower keeps the static rim; an airstrike becomes a number changing from 6 to 0 — the only event whose meaning still rides entirely on effects that are too thin.

## 4. What not to touch before 1.0

1. **Capture chain**: halo / flash ring / wipe / 0.85 squash / 8 chips / 6 px sin-decay shake / chime at +1 ms, settled ≈ 500 ms. It is the reference everything else is tuned against.
2. **Result timeline** (`src/render/resultTimeline.ts`): the 600 ms beat, card slide, `starLandMs` 561 / 821 / 1 081, gold 900–1 700, coin flights, toast release — the F9 cues hang off it.
3. **Press ring on touch-down** (F12) and **muster** (F5b): the two fixes that took Responsiveness and Dead Time to 2. Do not reintroduce a sim-side first-emission change without a full reclock (GDD §2.3 AUD-F5).
4. **Budgets**: shared landing bucket (8 burst, 40 s⁻¹), 700-particle cap, ≤ 2 fills per particle; the `hit` limiter (250 ms per tower, 170 ms global); per-sound rate limits.
5. **No global hit-stop.** In a real-time board with dozens of landings it reads as lag; the only authored freeze is the result beat.
6. **Mixer**: limiter (−6 dBFS, 20:1) and the −6 dB duck with its holds; new recipes go through `Synth.route`, never straight to the master.
7. **Reduced-motion substitutes** (static halo, rim, frost / gold rims, instant card) — every new effect above must add its own, not remove these.
8. **Eager entry**: ≈ 0.26 KiB left. Anything not a hook stays in `particlesEconomy` / `combatFx` / `hudOverlays` / the recipes or music chunks.
