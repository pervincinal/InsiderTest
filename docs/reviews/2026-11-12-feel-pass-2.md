- **Impact.** Airstrike now: anticipation (whistle, falling shell, darkening shadow 150 ms) → impact (flash, ring, soot, 3 px slam, thud on the same frame) → resolution (badge counting down over ≈ 115 ms). Booster cast: pop + glints + sound within one frame. Loss: thud, then sting, then the chord.
- **Rhythm.** See above; the stream → muster → march → landing → capture cadence is unchanged and good.
- **Clarity.** No cryptic moment found at 360 or 430. The active booster still shows its price chip (30) while running; the cooldown ring and the dark well read as "running", so I do not file it.
- **Payoff.** The win now has its own sound on the decisive frame; the airstrike pays for its 50 gold on screen.
- **Dead time.** None unintentional.

### Forcing questions

- **Q1, sound only.** I can now tell a win (+0 sting) from a capture, an airstrike (whistle → boom) from the enemy gun, and that I am losing (kick + hats enter). I still cannot hear that a *won* match was close. **Pass.**

- **Q3, 20th repetition.** Arrivals, sends and captures vary; the loop is a 19.2 s phrase. What wears first is still the music bed in matches I am winning.
- **Q4, no VFX.** The airstrike keeps its count-down number and its own sound; boosters keep their badge washes and the coloured active disc. Pass.
