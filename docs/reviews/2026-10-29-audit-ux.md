# Tower Clash — UI/UX, input and screens audit (2026-10-29)

Frontend Engineer, for the stakeholder's full audit with the new third-party skills. Skills applied: `gstack-game-ux-review` (rubrics from `references/scoring.md`, behavioural tests, mode A = Mobile / Touch), `game-ui-ux` (anchors, safe areas, scaling, screen stack, event-driven HUD), `input-systems` (touch mapping, hit areas, buffering, accessibility) and `camera-systems` (framing, bounds, the `?tilt=` perspective). Gamepad parts are skipped because the game does not support gamepads.

**Evidence.** Code at HEAD `90e2345`: `src/ui/`, `src/input/pointer.ts`, `src/render/{hud,draw,camera,view,layout,menuLayout,widgets}.ts`, `src/main.ts`; `docs/ART_DIRECTION.md` §2/§4–§6; `docs/GDD.md` §4–§6. I captured 74 frames from a scratch production build served on port 4190 (Chromium headless shell, DPR 2, touch via CDP). Phones: 360 × 640 with no insets, and 430 × 932 with iPhone safe areas 59 / 34. Languages: EN and AZ, plus RU for the play frames. Frame folder: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-InsiderTest/27ff3ffe-c8bd-51f2-b6a9-550752bc37bb/scratchpad/audit-ux/`; the capture script is in `…/scratchpad/audit-ux-tools/frames.cjs`. Frame names below leave out the folder. Capture artefact: the blue `×0.1` / `×10` speed tag in play frames comes from my debug speed changes (to freeze or fast-forward the sim) and is not a finding.

**Unit used for sizes.** All layout is in a 720 × 1280 logical frame that is letterboxed by width on every phone taller than 9:16. **1 logical px = 0.50 CSS px (= pt) at 360 wide, 0.597 at 430 wide.** "44 pt" therefore means **88 logical px at 360 and 74 at 430**. Most of the rects in `layout.ts` / `menuLayout.ts` were sized as if logical px were points. The comments say so: `TOWER_HIT_RADIUS` "44 pt", `LEVEL_MAP.yesterday` "≥ 44 px tap target" at 56 logical = 28 pt. This one mix-up is the root cause of AUD-U1 and AUD-U2.

## 5. Five things that work (and why)

1. **Garrison badges on the towers are the HUD.** The top-priority number for every decision sits on the object it describes, with the owner's rim and a ⚔ pip under fire. Nothing needs to be cross-referenced, which keeps the bottom band free for actions (`L25-t20-selected-*`). The pip is a shape, so it also survives the colour-blind palette.
2. **Safe-area and full-bleed handling follow game-ui-ux §2 closely.** Insets come from `env()` via CSS variables, are re-probed for 10 frames and on visibility change, never take more than half the viewport, and anchor the HUD bands to the safe edges while the glass bleeds under the notch. The 430 × 932 frames have no clipped control and no letterbox bars.
3. **"Press must begin on the button" (BUG-36/37) and the `pointercancel` recovery (BUG-33)** make the touch layer robust where WebKit is hostile. A slide off a button never fires it, a band press never leaks into the world, and an iOS-cancelled drag still lands. The `input-systems` "capture discrete presses as events" rule is met.
4. **The tutorial teaches by doing and never blocks.** Bubble plus arrow plus pulsing ring on the exact tower, with the step's selection preset. It times out instead of soft-locking, skips itself on replays, and leaves 0 forced words (`L1-t01/05/15-*`). Defeat brings back the level's lesson as a tip, and on a second loss links How to Play: help arrives at the moment of failure, not before.
5. **One design system with event-cached HUD painting.** Every screen uses the same `drawButton` / `drawPill` / `drawCard` clay widgets and palette tokens, so a player who learns "blue = primary" on the title can rely on it elsewhere. The HUD canvas redraws only when `hudKey` changes, which is the game-ui-ux "drive the HUD from changes, not every frame" intent applied to a canvas game, at no per-frame raster cost.
