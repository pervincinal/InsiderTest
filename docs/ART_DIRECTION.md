# Art Direction v2 — "Sunlit Clay Islands"

Stakeholder feedback (2026-09-13): the first Tower War-style pass reads as flat clip-art. Target: **premium casual** — the 2025–26 trend of "premium minimalism": simple shapes executed with real material quality, soft-3D (claymorphism) UI, strong lighting, confident typography and orchestrated motion (sources: casual game art trend reports 2025–26, UI trend guides on claymorphism/glassmorphism). Everything is still canvas 2D primitives, no images.

## 1. Mood
Sunny afternoon over toy islands. Everything looks moulded from soft clay: rounded, matte, three tones per object (lit / mid / shade) plus a thin rim light. One warm key light from the **upper-left** (about 35° elevation); every object drops a **long, soft, slightly blue shadow** to the lower-right onto the ground. Colours are saturated but harmonised; no pure black, no pure white.

- **Lighting model** (ART-23, 2026-10-13, after TestFlight build 12: "Rəng effektləri o deyil" — read as "the picture is flat, combat moments have weak effects"): the one key light is **baked**, nothing is lit per frame. On the ground (terrain.ts `lightPlateau`, once per level, inside the plateau clip, under the sun patches): a **warm pool** of sunlight over the upper-left third of the playfield (three nested ellipses of `BIOME_LIGHT[b].warm`, together ≈ `warmAlpha` — clay-style stepped pools like the sun patches, not lost in the far haze), a **cool wash** of blue ink from beyond the near / lower-right corner (two nested ellipses, ≈ `coolAlpha`) and a **vignette** (two wide translucent strokes of the outline darken the rim, `vignette`). Solid translucent fills only: a gradient or a scaled image over the island costs 5–10× a solid fill in the software rasteriser (measured), and the ground is rebuilt on every level start (e2e/perf.spec.ts ground build). The cliff face is warm on the light's side and cool on the far side (one gradient wash clipped to the near shore's band, the only place a cliff face shows) with a bright line under the grass lip (its lit top edge) over the existing dark under-cliff band; the sea's outer drop-shadow pass reaches further out on the shadow side (deeper water there), and a column of warm **sun glitter** dashes sits on the left, under the light, densest at the horizon (`paintGlitter`, own rng). Per biome (`BIOME_LIGHT` in palette.ts): grass / autumn butter light; sand bright with a warm-brown shade (a blue shade turned it grey next to the neutral towers); snow cream light and blue shade; volcanic a faint ember glow, little shade; twilight a rosy light (amber greyed the violet) and the lightest shade so the ribbons' ink outline keeps ≥ 3:1. `tests/render/light.test.ts` composites the extremes (`litGround`) and holds every owner mid tone of both palettes within 8 ΔE of its unlit distance and ≥ ΔE 21 from the ground. **Clay owner tones**: the default cone roof gets a hot spot on the lit facet, a rim light along the lit eave in `rimOf(tones)` (= `shade(lit, 0.5)`) and a dark occlusion line under the shaded eave (`roofLight`, baked in the PERF-4 sprite cache; shaped / striped / slate roofs, the app icon and store art keep their look); the flag's lower fold is in the shade tone (lit stripe / mid / shade); the badge paper gets a lower lip in `shade(paper, −BADGE_LIP)` (ink ≥ 4.5:1 on it). **Streams** glow: one wide translucent stroke in the owner's lit tone under every ribbon (`LINK_GLOW_PX` 16, `LINK_GLOW_ALPHA` 0.2).

## 3. World
- **Camera — default: flat and zoomed in** (ART-24, 2026-10-13, stakeholder on build 14: "Görünüşü helikopter view et, amma yaxınlaşdır və qalalar, oyunçular daha böyük olsun. Qalalar arasında məsafənin az görünməsi okdir." — top-down again, but closer, towers and soldiers bigger; towers looking closer together is fine): the play world is the flat 720 × 1280 map seen straight down (`camera.ts` `TILT_DEFAULT` = 1, the identity — no squash, no horizon haze or islets, the old wave bands and cliff), with everything standing drawn larger than its design size about its own ground anchor (`layout.ts` `WORLD_ZOOM`; map, levels and sim untouched): **buildings × 1.3** (through the canvas transform, so the PERF-4 sprite cache bakes them crisply at the one zoomed scale) with their shadows, plinth / selection / streaming rings and airstrike rings; **soldiers × 1.75, tanks × 1.5** (`SOLDIER_DRAW_SCALE` / `TANK_DRAW_SCALE`, was 1.45 / 1.2); **props × 1.25** (bushes, pines, cacti, decor rocks, flower dots, mine bodies and lamps); **walls' drawn thickness and boulders × 1.2** (collision unchanged); **badges and their stream / mine chips × 1.15** (numerals stay ≥ 24 px; a chip that cannot slide clear of a zoomed badge now steps beside it, right then left — a mine just above a keep put its charge chip on the keep's count on levels 40 / 50); **stream ribbons 10 → 13 px** with their chevrons, pitch and arrowhead, their ends clearing the zoomed bases; **guide lines 2 → 2.5 px**. Tap targets follow the drawn buildings: `TOWER_HIT_RADIUS` 44 → 57 and the base-to-roof capsule (`hitTower`, BUG-41) on the flat map too. **Hit resolution** (BUG-44, 2026-10-14): a tap inside exactly one tower's base circle takes that tower; inside several, the nearest base; on a drawn body only (|dx| ≤ zoomed plinth radius, roof ≤ y ≤ base), the one painted last (largest base y); in a capsule's margin, the nearest base. **Badge placement** (BUG-42 / BUG-43, 2026-10-14, `worldChips.ts` `placeBadges`, pure and shared with `tests/render/badgeLayout.test.ts`): towers sorted by y then id; a badge whose above-the-roof spot is clamped under the top band / lesson card by ≤ ⅓ of its height stays there and is fixed first; every other badge takes the first free spot of below the base → right of the base → left → the clamped spot above → (base itself under the card) beside at the clamped height → the clamped spot anyway; "free" = inside the map and the play band, 6 px from the lesson card, 3 px from every badge placed so far. Stream / mine chips lay out against the final rects; the refusal bubble opens under a badge that is not above its tower. The title island and the shop previews keep their own scales (`TerrainSpec.zoom` is set by the play screen only). Lighting (ART-23 ground light, cliff, sea glitter, roof highlights, combat effects) is camera-independent and stays.
- **Camera — perspective override** (`?tilt=0.72`, or any k in 0.6..1, and `setTilt()`; `?tilt=0` is the flat map) — ART-21 v2, 2026-10-12, after TestFlight build 12: "Oyun helikopter view kimidir" — the field read as a flat map seen straight down; the default in builds 13–14, kept for A/B: the play world is seen through a perspective camera looking across the island toward a horizon, `src/render/camera.ts`. It is a **plane homography** of the ground, so straight lanes stay straight (ribbons, guide lines and the drag band are drawn between projected end points and the units walking them stay on them): depth scale `s(y) = 1 / (1 + g·(1180 − y))` is 1 at the bottom of the play band and **k = 0.72** at its far edge (y 96); a map point is drawn at `X = 360 + (x − 360)·s`, `Y = horizon + span·s` (horizon ≈ −2384, far above the screen). The far shore (map y 108) lands on logical y **190** — about 70 CSS px of horizon sea under the lesson card on a 430 × 932 phone, under the card on 9:16 — and the near edge stays where the flat map had it. Down the ground the screen scale runs from 0.66 (far) to 1.28 (near) px per map px: the far side is foreshortened and narrowed (a trapezoid), the near side opened up so the island fills the band. **The ground** (island, cliff, foam, sun patches, bevel, rivers, walls, craters, wave bands, aerial haze) is painted flat on a map-space canvas once per level and warped into the terrain cache row by row ("mode 7": one `drawImage` per 3-device-row band with that band's x-scale about the centre column, low smoothing; nothing per frame. BUG-40: the map-space canvas holds only the island at 1 device px per map px and is reused by back-to-back builds, the wave bands are drawn through the camera onto the cache and the props in a second rng-identical pass straight onto it — level 40 builds in ≈ 80–85 ms at 4× CPU, was 206–275); the sea gradient, horizon haze and islets are painted under it in screen space, the props over it. **Standing things** are drawn upright at the projected anchor and scaled by `s`: buildings (the PERF-4 sprite cache bakes at the base scale and blits resampled with a draw-time scale — no new keys), soldiers and tanks (size × s, heading projected), building shadows and the selection / streaming rings (in the sprite's own 0.42 perspective), props, mine bodies and lamps, every world particle (puffs, sparks, impact rings, planks — one transform each; confetti and purchase coins stay in screen space; shell tracers project both ends), the artillery barrel's aim. **Readability floors**: badges shrink with depth no further than 24 / 26 (`BADGE_MIN_SCALE`: numerals ≥ 24 px, ART-17), stream chips, mine charge chips, the refusal bubble, the tutorial arrow / bubble and the guide / drag line widths keep their size (positioned by depth); ribbon widths use the mean depth of their ends. **Depth cues**: the cliff face is 1.8× taller per unit of (1 − k) (30 → 45 px), walls stand `wallHeight` = 18 px × depth (lifted top face over a darker two-tone front face with a mortar course; 4 px lip when flat), the far 520 map px of the plateau fade into the haze (aerial perspective, 36 % at the far shore), cloud shadows drift along their projected rows narrowed and flattened, and above the far shore the sea turns into a pale **horizon haze** (`shade(waterTop, 0.62)` at 72 %, full from logical y 40 up, painted identically by `sea.ts` in the full-bleed margins and by the terrain cache so no seam shows) with three or four small low islets that get smaller and paler with distance; the distant sea has no wave bands or sparkles. Not tilted: the HUD bands, overlays (pause / result), menus and the title island. Input stays exact: `toLogical` gives the screen logical point, the play screen undoes the camera (`unproj`: y from the row, then x) before the gestures, tower hit areas follow the drawn building — a screen capsule from its base up to its roof top (`towerTop × s`) of radius `TOWER_HIT_RADIUS × max(1, s)`, so a far tower never drops below the flat 44 pt and a tap on its roof selects it; where capsules overlap the nearest base wins (`hitTower(…, project)`, QA-23), and the debug hooks `toClient` / `layoutToClient` project map points of the play band while the game is not paused (HUD and pause-card points pass through). The level-1 tutorial bubble moves beside its tower (wrapped, arrow pointing sideways) when above it would cover another tower. `?tilt=0` in the URL is the exact old flat map (s ≡ 1), `?tilt=0.65` etc. sets k (0.6..1) for A/B screenshots.

Decorations (bushes, rocks, sun patches) keep off obstacles, mines and tower footprints.

## 4. UI (claymorphism)
- **Typeface**: Fredoka (Google Fonts, OFL) 700 for numerals/headings, 500 for labels; files in `public/fonts/`, `@font-face` in `index.html`, canvas waits for `document.fonts.ready`. Fallback: system rounded sans.
- **Buttons/pills**: paper fill, 18 px radius, **inner highlight** top (1 px lighter), **outer soft shadow** (0 6 px, 12 % ink), 4 px coloured bottom edge for primary (player blue) / secondary (paper with ink shade). Pressed: move down 3 px, edge shrinks. Text ink or paper on colour.
- **HUD**: top-left level chip (small "LEVEL 9" over name), centre timer pill with ink numerals, right pause button; bottom: SEND ratio segmented toggle (100 % | 50 %) and MENU. HUD sits on a subtle translucent paper band with 10 % ink blur look (glass-like) so it reads over any biome.
- **Result card**: clay card slides up with a bounce; three stars pop in sequentially with a gold glow burst; "VICTORY" as extruded 3D lettering (layered offsets) in gold with ink outline; coins count up; buttons row.
- **Pause card**: same card style, smaller.
- **Title**: island vignette, logo "TOWER CLASH" as extruded 3D clay letters (paper + blue), soft floating animation, a big primary PLAY, small settings row; ambient soldiers marching between demo towers.
- **Level select**: a **winding path map** down a long island (like Royal Match/Candy Crush): round clay nodes with the level number, cleared = gold with stars beneath, current = pulsing with a flag, locked = grey with a lock; the path scrolls vertically (drag/wheel), starts scrolled to the current level. Coins pill at the top.

## 5. Motion (all respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: nothing below spawns, no badge flash, no stream pulse)
- Capture: tower squashes (0.85 y) and pops back, roof colour swaps with a radial wipe, 6 px screen nudge (player captures); ART-23: the owner ring plus a **flash ring** in the new owner's lit tone racing out from the plinth (420 ms, paper core line), a paper flash at the base, **12 clay chips** in the new owner's lit / mid tones (paper for every fourth) thrown sideways out of the roof eave and showing their shade face as they tumble (they used to be 28 unprojected confetti flying up through the badge), 8 sparks, and a **badge halo** in the new owner's lit tone (`captureFlash`, 320 ms, numerals on top).
- Landing (ART-23): a hostile landing = a bigger impact ring in the attacker's colour, a hit flash in its lit tone and 4 sparks (attacker mid / lit / paper), at most once per tower per 90 ms — on owned towers from the "under fire" deadline (with the badge jolt), on neutral towers (never under fire; before ART-23 they showed nothing) from the sim's `landed` event; a reinforcement (`landed`, not hostile) = a faint small ring and 3 sparks in the owner's lit tone, at most once per tower per 160 ms. Both take a token from one shared bucket (8 burst, 40 / s), so a 400-unit late game spawns ≤ 40 small bursts a second.
- Stream start (ART-23): a paper pulse runs from the source to the target down a new ribbon once (`STREAM_PULSE_MS` 380, ease-out, from the first frame the link is drawn — draw.ts `linkPulse`); the source's streaming ground ring is brighter (mid 42–72 %, lit 50–80 %).
- Upgrade: gold gem glints, roof scales 1.15 → 1, gold ring at the base; ART-23: a **sparkle fountain** of 14 gold / pale-gold / paper glints shooting up from the roof in two plumes either side of the badge column and falling back (≈ 0.6–0.9 s).
- Send: source badge pops, units spawn with a small scale-in.
- Artillery: muzzle flash, tracer, hit spark.
- Victory: confetti + star sequence; defeat: desaturate world slightly, card slides in.

## 6. Performance & readability
- 400 units at 60 fps on a mid phone: per-unit drawing is ≤ 12 fills / strokes (ART-20: shadow · legs ink + tone · outline · shade side · body · lit facet · spear shaft · spear head · face + fist · eyes · helmet; the shield-bearer and robot skins stay within the same 12; `tests/render/march.test.ts` counts them), no save/restore or transforms per unit, no per-frame gradients on units (pre-tinted), shadows are flat ellipses.
- Numerals ≥ 24 px logical, contrast ≥ 4.5:1 on badges; owner colours distinguishable in both palettes.
- ART-23 budget: the lighting is baked (terrain cache, sprite cache) — 0 per-frame cost; per frame it adds one stroke per stream (glow), one per new stream (pulse) and one per badge (lip). Combat particles are ≤ 2 fills / strokes each, rate-limited as above (≤ ≈ 250 alive at 400 units, cap 700); eager bundle +1.4 kB gzip; the level-start ground build gains ≈ 4 ms (unthrottled sandbox, ≈ 10 %).

## 7. App icon (ART-18, 2026-10-11)
- **Brief**: reads at 60 px on a busy home-screen wallpaper; one bold silhouette, warm saturated field, no text or numbers.
- **Shipped: "Sunburst"** — the hero clay tower (the game's plinth / cylinder / cone / flag / gem primitives from `sprites.ts`, chunkier: oversized player-blue cone roof, big blue flag, three gold level gems) on a small grass plateau, two crossed steel swords fanning out behind the roof (the clash), on gold-amber rays around a pale sun core that deepen to orange at the edges.
